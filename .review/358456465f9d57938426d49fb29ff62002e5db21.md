I stopped a leftover wait loop. It only watched the review workflow's progress, and that workflow had already finished. The review report above is complete and has not changed: four Major findings and no Blocker.

1. **macOS restart:** after the app restarts, uploads of files the person picked or dropped cannot resume, because the app keeps no lasting access to the file. Depending on the case, the entry either fails for good or retries forever.
2. **Downloads:** downloads and thumbnails skip the DPoP nonce retry, the token refresh and the sign-out when the session is revoked. A short idle period is enough to make thumbnails fail and stay failed.
3. **Ticket URL:** when playback fails, the audioplayers package prints the file ticket URL to the console. This breaks the rule that ticket URLs are never logged.
4. **Voice recording:** a double click on Stop, or Stop at the same moment as the automatic stop, marks a good voice recording as failed. The only button left then deletes it.

Urteil: NEIN 4 Majors: macOS sandbox breaks upload resume after restart, downloads bypass DPoP nonce/refresh/revocation handling, ticket URL printed by audioplayers logger, double stop() deletes a good voice recording
