### Added

- Status page (M7-09, 16 section 12, R-OP-15): `getStatus`, `getPublicStatus` (only with
  `status.public_enabled`, otherwise `404`), `createStatusComponent`, `updateStatusComponent`,
  `deleteStatusComponent`, `createStatusIncident` and `addStatusIncidentUpdate`, with the tables
  `status_components`, `status_incidents`, `status_incident_updates` and
  `status_incident_components`. Components of an agent are shown only with `agent.read` and never
  count for the overall state; every change appends `status.updated`.
- The job `status.probe` on the queue `maintenance` (every `status.check_interval` and at the start
  of a worker) keeps the standard components `api`, `push`, `files` and `search` and one component
  per active agent, and checks them from inside the server: the error ratios and the fan-out lag of
  the own measurement in `stats_minutes`, a probe object of the object storage, the `PING` of
  clamd, a test query of the search and the stored agent presence. Failing checks give `degraded`
  or `major_outage` after `status.degrade_after`; a `major_outage` longer than
  `status.incident_after` opens an automatic incident, resolved after `status.resolve_after` of
  health. A disabled agent's component goes with `agent.disable`.
- The job `retention.ops` removes incidents resolved before `retention.ops` and old backup runs,
  keeping the latest successful run of each kind.
- Configuration: `status.check_interval`, `status.degrade_after`, `status.incident_after`,
  `status.resolve_after`, `status.error_ratio_degraded`, `status.error_ratio_outage`,
  `status.fanout_lag_degraded`, `status.public_enabled`, `status.history_window` and
  `retention.ops` are active; the cross-check `status_error_ratios` keeps the two ratios in order.
