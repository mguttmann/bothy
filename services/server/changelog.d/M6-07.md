### Added

- Permission hints and expiring access (M6-07, 07 sections 9.4 and 9.5, R-AZ-34): the operations
  `listPermissionHints` (`GET /api/v1/access/hints`) and `listExpiringAccess`
  (`GET /api/v1/access/expiring`), action `access.hints.read`, for owners on the instance and for
  managers within a space (no read `404`, read without `manage` `403`). The hints are H1 (a
  person of an agent's trigger set who cannot read a channel of the agent's read horizon, the
  same function as `via_agent` of "who has access"), grants to disabled principals, `trigger`
  grants that SR2 or SR3 keep from triggering, and agents in mode `personal` with their trigger
  set, computed from the permission state of `authorize` and paged by kind and key. "Expiring
  soon" lists grants, memberships and, for owners, agent and app tokens expiring within
  `authz.expiry_reminder_lead`, ordered by expiry, never a token value. Both pages carry the names
  of what they name. New source ports `authz.AgentDirectory` (agents) and
  `authz.TokenExpirySource` (identity).
