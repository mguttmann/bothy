### Added

- Images without metadata and thumbnails (M3-06, Ruling L16, ADR 0047): a clean or skipped JPEG,
  PNG, GIF or WebP stays `pending` after the scan until the new job `files.thumbnail` (queue
  `files`, River timeout `file.image.processing_timeout`, at most `file.image.max_concurrent` per
  worker) stored its cleaned original as revision 2 and deleted revision 1. JPEG and PNG are
  filtered losslessly with an allow list (only the orientation as EXIF tag, the ICC profile
  unchanged, no GPS, XMP, IPTC, comments, embedded thumbnails or appended images); a broken
  structure fails the file with `image_malformed`. GIF is re-encoded, a still WebP becomes PNG or
  JPEG (`file.image.jpeg_quality`), animated WebP and other types stay unchanged. The job renders
  `small`, `medium` and `large` (`file.thumbnail.*`) in pure Go with the orientation applied and
  the colour profile of the original, never decodes above `file.image.max_pixels`, and reads
  width, height and duration of MP4 and QuickTime files from their boxes. New: the rows of
  `file_thumbnails` (the table of M3-05), `File.thumbnails` in `getFile` and `file.updated`, the
  start check `thumbnail_sizes_ordered`, and `golang.org/x/image` (only in
  `internal/files/adapters/imaging`).
