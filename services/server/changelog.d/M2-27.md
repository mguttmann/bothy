### Added

- Incoming webhooks (M2-27): migration `00055_apps_incoming_webhooks.sql` (table
  `incoming_webhooks`), the port `apps.IncomingWebhooks` and the operations
  `listIncomingWebhooks`, `createIncomingWebhook`, `revokeIncomingWebhook`, `postIncomingWebhook`
  (token as the last path segment) and `postIncomingWebhookWithBearer` (`Authorization: Bearer`).
  A manager of the channel creates a webhook for an installed app with `write` there (step-up,
  audit; `400 validation_failed` with `app_not_installed`, `app_cannot_write` or
  `not_in_channel`) and gets the absolute URL from `server.public_url` with a `bothy_whi_` token
  once; only its HMAC with the token pepper is stored, and a replay of the `Idempotency-Key`
  answers `409 conflict` `one_time_secret`. A post passes `rl.webhook_in` per webhook, the token
  check (the new chain stage `httpserver.StageWebhookToken` after the rate limit; a failure takes
  one token of `rl.public_auth` of the client IP), the body limit, the format and `authorize` of
  `webhook_in.post` for the app, in this order; an unknown webhook, a wrong or malformed token, a
  revoked webhook, an installation that is not active and every refusal of the app's rights give
  the same `404 not_found`. The format is recognised per request: `native` (`content_md`,
  `title`, `thread_id`, `client_msg_id`; a repeated `client_msg_id` answers `200` with
  `Idempotent-Replay: true`, other content `422 idempotency_key_reuse`) or the unchanged
  Alertmanager payload (schema `AlertmanagerAlert`), rendered into one message per notification
  with escaped values and only absolute `http` and `https` URLs; a mixed body is `format_mixed`, a
  switched-off format `format_disabled`. The target is the fixed thread of the webhook, else
  `thread_id` of the body in the same channel, else a new thread with `title` (in a text channel
  from the message in the main thread), else the main thread (a forum without one is
  `required_for_forum_post`). The message has `origin = app` with the app as author, resolves no
  mentions and triggers no agent. Both post operations are also served on the internal listener
  in every role, through a chain of their own with the peer address as client IP. Uninstalling the
  app and archiving the channel revoke its webhooks; a suspended installation refuses posts until
  resume. `messaging.CreateMessageCmd` gains `SkipMentions` and `HashContext`, `identity.Tokens`
  gains `NewIncomingWebhookToken` and `HashIncomingWebhookToken`, chat gains the port
  `ThreadsByParent` and `TriggerChannel.MainThreadID`, `httpserver.Operation` gains `BodyLimit`,
  `BodyLimitKey` and `AnonymousCredentials`, and `ratelimit.Limiter` gains
  `FailedCredentialGuard`. New keys `webhook_in.max_body_bytes` and `webhook_in.formats_enabled`
  with the start check `webhook_in_formats` (only `native` and `alertmanager`, each at most once).
