### Added

- Wake-ups of stage AR (AR-04, docs/spec/09-agents.md 5.8): the tables `run_wakes` and
  `run_autonomy`, the trigger kind `wake` with `runs.triggering_wake_id` and the channel switch
  `channels.allow_autonomous_runs` (migrations 00097 to 00101, expand only, every new column "as
  before"). The operations `createRunWake`, `recoverRunWake`, `listPendingRunWakes`,
  `checkRunWake`, `fireRunWake`, `cancelRunWake`, `listThreadWakes`, `checkRunLaunch` and
  `setChannelAutonomousRuns`; the durable events `run.wake_changed` and `agent.wake_input` (with a
  token of trigger kind `wake` and the claim `wake_id`); the job `runs.wake_due`.
- A fired wake-up starts a wake run, or a wake input into the running run of its person, with the
  checks of a new run; the caps `run.autonomous_chain_max` and `run.autonomous_idle_max` per thread
  and person, `run.wake_min_delay`, `run.wake_max_pending`, `run.wake_max_wait` and
  `run.usage_wake_max`; every end other than `fired` posts a note in the thread, and `refused`,
  `expired` and `failed` notify the person with the new kind `wake_ended`.
- The heartbeat accepts the capabilities `turn_handover` and `wakes`; `usage_limited` is a reason
  of the adapter (09 5.2) and gives `wake_ended` instead of `run_failed`; the patch member
  `/metrics/autonomy_last` and `autonomy_cap_reached` in the patch answer.
- Metrics `bothy.run.wake.pending`, `bothy.run.wake.ended` and `bothy.run.autonomy.cap_reached`.
- Nothing changes for an installation whose runners do not report `wakes`. Before a rollback below
  this version let pending wake-ups fire or cancel them (runs README).
