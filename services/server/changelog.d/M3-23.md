### Added

- Desktop hints over the gateway (M3-23, 13 4.4, 4.6, 6.3 and 12): the gateway implements
  `presence.View` over its hub (one entry per device with a live human connection, with a copy of
  the focus and the `idle` flag), and in role `all` the push path uses it with the live routes of
  `notify`: a gateway route becomes one unique job `notify.gateway_send` per hint and recipient,
  which checks the rights of that moment after the commit and publishes `notification.created` to
  the recipient's live connections; a push delayed while a desktop is active is a
  `notify.push_send` at the end of `notify.mobile_delay_when_desktop_active` that ends with the
  skip reason `read` when the person read the message meanwhile. A thread in focus on the desktop
  gives no push to the phone. `sync.Admit` admits a hint without a thread (resource `user`) only to
  the recipient's own connections. The payload of `notification.created` gains the optional
  member `target_id` (`openapi.yaml`, generated clients). Role `worker` keeps `presence.None` and
  logs one Info line; separate roles reach desktops by push until M7-17.
