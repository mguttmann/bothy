### Added

- Own measurement (M6-13, docs/spec/25-visualization.md 3, R-VZ-02, R-VZ-04): the port
  `kernel/measure` (`Record(metric, key, value)` with the 18 metrics, their closed dimension
  lists and scopes) and its implementation in `stats`: counters per minute and key in memory,
  flushed every `stats.flush_interval` by an additive upsert into the new table `stats_minutes`
  (migration `00075`, partitioned per UTC day, unique key over all dimensions with `NULLS NOT
  DISTINCT`, no column for people) and once at the shutdown; beyond `stats.flush_queue_max` keys
  or after a failed write the oldest are dropped and counted in `bothy.stats.flush.dropped`
  (`queue_full`, `db_error`, `invalid_key`), every flush in `bothy.stats.flush.duration`. The
  measuring points sit next to their OpenTelemetry instruments: `messages_created` and
  `threads_created` (messaging), `runs_started`, `runs_finished`, `tool_calls`, `approval_cards`,
  `invocations_denied`, `cost`, `sli.run_start` and `sli.turn_duration` (runs),
  `billing_mode_switches` (agents), `sli.push_acceptance` (notify), `catchups`, `resyncs`,
  `slow_consumer_closes`, `sli.delivery_latency` and `sli.reconnect` (gateway) and
  `sli.api_availability` (the HTTP chain); domain counters only after the commit, SLI events good
  up to and including the threshold of `slibounds.gen.go`. The job `stats.retention` (queue
  `maintenance`, hourly and at the start) creates today's and tomorrow's partition and drops
  partitions older than `retention.stats_minutes`; `bothy config check` refuses a retention shorter
  than the longest SLO period. New keys `stats.flush_interval`, `stats.flush_queue_max` and
  `retention.stats_minutes`; `make ops-check` (`sli-labels`) also holds the `sli.*` constants of
  `kernel/measure` equal to the SLO labels.
