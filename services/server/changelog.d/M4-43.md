### Added

- New project from the app (M4-43): `project_bindings` gets `provision` (`none`, `init`, `clone`)
  and `ready_reason` (migrations 00082 and 00083). `putProjectBinding` takes `provision`; with
  `init` or `clone` the `workspace_ref` must be one segment of letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-`
  that starts with a letter or digit, and `clone` needs an `https` `repository_url` without query
  or fragment. A change of `provision` is a change of the binding. The heartbeat entry
  `project_readiness` takes an optional `reason` (a code, only with `ready` false); the server
  stores it as `ready_reason`, applies a change of the reason alone like a change of readiness, and
  clears it with `ready` true, with every effective binding change and with an operator change.
  `ProjectBinding` always carries `provision` and carries `ready_reason` while one is set.
