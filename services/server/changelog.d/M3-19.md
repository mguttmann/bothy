### Added

- Hint jobs, automatic following and bundling (M3-19, 13 sections 2 to 4, 7.3, 8 and 12): the
  table `push_registrations` (migration 00057, retention class `security`) with the read port
  `notify.PushRegistrations.Active`, which never returns the token. The worker consumer `push`
  turns `message.created`, `run.state_changed`, `access.expiring` and `device.pending` into hint
  jobs in the round that moves its cursor, with candidates from the new port
  `authz.Recipients.Readers`, mentions and run starters, each decided with `push.deliver` in
  filter mode; agent messages during an active run give one hint at the end of the run, and
  `read_state.updated` schedules the badge sync. `identity` writes the durable event
  `device.pending` for a device registered as `pending` (R-AZ-31), the source of `security`
  hints to the other active devices of that human. Bundling per recipient and thread lives in
  the unique jobs `notify.coalesce_window` and `notify.coalesce_flush`; the job
  `notify.push_send` checks registration, age, rights and provider, sends through the provider
  port `PushProvider` outside any transaction under `push.max_concurrent_requests`, applies the
  result classes of 13 7.3 and ends with `no_provider` while no provider is registered; every push
  carries the badge number of 13 8, and `notify.badge_sync` with `notify.badge_sync_hold` sends at
  most one badge sync per person per `notify.badge_sync_min_interval`. A human who writes in a
  thread follows it with level `all` unless an own setting exists. New: the kernel port
  `presence.View` with `presence.None`, the unique inserts `InsertUniqueTx` and `PendingTx` of
  `platform/jobs`, the reads `sync.History`, `sync.ReadPositions`, `chat.Threads.UnreadCandidates`,
  `identity.Devices.ActiveForUsers` and four reads of `messaging.Messages`, the metrics
  `bothy.push.sent`, `bothy.push.send.duration`, `bothy.push.skipped` (without `quiet_hours`) and
  `bothy.push.registrations.active`, the span `push.send` and the configuration keys
  `notify.coalesce_window`, `notify.mobile_delay_when_desktop_active`,
  `notify.default_admin_enabled`, `notify.default_input_needed_breaks_quiet_hours`,
  `notify.badge_sync_min_interval`, `push.max_age`, `push.retry_base`, `push.retry_cap`,
  `push.max_concurrent_requests` and `push.max_consecutive_failures`.
