### Added

- Read mirror of the import tool (M8-05, 22 12): `bothy import discord --apply --follow` copies, every
  `import.follow.poll_interval`, the settled new Discord messages of the channels whose stored
  lead is `discord`, posts new threads and mirrors renames, archiving and reopening;
  `--apply --once` runs one round for scheduled calls (exit `3` while another run holds the lease);
  without `--apply` a round only reads and reports what it would copy. The settle rule holds back
  busy (`import.follow.busy_reactions`) and unsettled (`import.follow.settle_after`) messages and
  checks `import.follow.busy_lookback` messages before the first new one. A failed round waits with
  backoff up to `import.follow.max_backoff` and never ends the mirror, the lease stays fresh during
  every wait, an error of one thread does not hold up the others, a channel with lead `bothy` that
  shows new Discord messages gets a warning and nothing is copied, and SIGINT or SIGTERM finish the
  current batch and clear the lease. The report counts `messages_held`.
