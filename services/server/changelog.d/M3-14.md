### Added

- Full-text search and the prefix search of the quick switcher (M3-14, 14 sections 4, 7, 10 and
  11, R-SU-03 to R-SU-06, R-SU-09): `GET /api/v1/search` (`search`) and `GET
  /api/v1/search/thread-titles` (`searchThreadTitles`) are enabled (`search.query`, `rl.search`).
  The new module `search` owns no tables: it takes the visible channels from `Authorizer.Scope`
  (cut by `view_as` and by the triggering person of a run, SR9), lets `messaging`, `chat` and
  `files` run one statement per search through their new read operations (`SearchMessages`,
  `SearchThreadTitles`, `SearchFiles`, `PrefixThreadTitles`, `ThreadsMatching`,
  `ChannelsInSpaces`, `Labels`, `HitMessages`, `HitThreads`, `HitFiles`), ranks the newest
  `search.max_candidates` candidates, highlights the page as text segments split at random
  markers, pages with a cursor bound to the request, the principal and the reference time of the
  first page, and checks every delivered hit with `authorize` again. There is no total and no
  count per channel; `candidates_capped` reports the candidate limit. A too long `q` answers `400
  validation_failed` with `too_long`, a `q` without a term with `empty`, a filter outside its
  types with `not_applicable`, and a statement that reaches `search.statement_timeout` `503
  unavailable` with `reason` `search_timeout`.
- The shared library `internal/kernel/searchkit`, the option `txn.StatementTimeout` of the unit of
  work with `pg.IsStatementTimeout`, the instrument `bothy.search.duration` with `bothy.outcome`
  (`ok`, `client_error`, `timeout`, `server_error`), and the configuration keys
  `search.query_max_chars`, `search.max_candidates`, `search.rank_normalization`,
  `search.recency_scale`, `search.page_size_default`, `search.page_size_max`,
  `search.headline_input_max_chars`, `search.headline_max_fragments`, `search.headline_max_words`,
  `search.headline_min_words`, `search.prefix_min_chars` and `search.statement_timeout` with the
  start checks `search_page_size` and `search_headline_words`.
