### Added

- Run reason `adapter_stopped` (09 5.2): an adapter reports `finishRun` `cancelled` with it when
  its own orderly stop ended the run's turn. It is no reason of a cancel request; open
  follow-ups of such a run are deferred like after every `cancelled` reason other than
  `user_cancelled`. The metric `bothy.run.finished` counts it as its own `bothy.reason`.
