### Added

- Cleanup and deletion of files (M3-08, 12 section 12, 20 4.3, DP-T01 for files): deleting a
  message, `deleteFile` and the release of an avatar or logo mark the files deleted, abort an open
  upload session, redact the payloads of their stored `file.updated` events (R-DM-45) and queue the
  new job `files.purge_objects`, which deletes the originals, the four variants and the parts or
  the multipart upload by their known keys and then the row (R-DM-46). A transition to `failed`,
  `infected` or `error` queues the same job in its transaction. The new periodic job
  `files.cleanup_pending` (every `file.gc_interval` and at worker start) expires open sessions
  past `file.upload_session_ttl`, deletes never attached files after `file.unattached_ttl` unless
  a draft of their uploader holds them, deletes the rows of unattached failed or blocked files
  after `file.failed_row_ttl`, catches up failed purges, removes stale temporary files of the file
  system driver and records the gauge `bothy.file.purge.backlog`. `deleteFile` is enabled: `204`
  for an own unattached file and for a file the caller already deleted, `404` for an unknown,
  foreign or purged file, `409 conflict` with `reason` `file_attached` or
  `purpose_not_deletable`. New configuration key `file.gc_batch_size`, the keys
  `file.unattached_ttl`, `file.failed_row_ttl`, `file.gc_interval` and
  `privacy.object_delete_delay` are now read, and the cross-check `object_delete_delay_gc` holds
  `file.gc_interval` below `privacy.object_delete_delay`.
