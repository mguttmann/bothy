### Added

- Privacy requests (M7-06, 20 section 5): `listGdprRequests`, `createGdprRequest`,
  `getGdprRequest`, `decideGdprRequest` and `downloadGdprExport` with step-up for the writes and
  the download. A person files an export, deletion or rectification request for themselves, an
  owner for anybody; `due_at` is the receipt plus one calendar month (a day missing in the target
  month moves to its last day), and `decideGdprRequest` may extend it once by one or two months
  before it expires (`extend_months`). One request per person and kind may be open (`409` naming
  the open request); the deletion of the last active owner is `409 last_owner`.
- The export of one's own data is approved by the system unless
  `privacy.export_requires_approval`; the job `privacy.export` (queue `privacy`) builds the ZIP of
  20 5.2 from the participants of every module, with `README.txt` in German or English and
  `manifest.json` with the SHA-256 of every file, stores it as a file with purpose `gdpr_export`
  and serves it for `privacy.export_ttl`; the job `privacy.export_expiry` deletes it afterwards
  and the download answers `410 gone` (DP-T03, DP-T04). The format is described by JSON Schemas
  in `docs/api/gdpr-export/v1/`, checked by `make api-checks`.
- The account deletion: the job `privacy.erase` runs the steps of 20 4.4 through the new kernel
  port `personaldata` (one participant per module with personal data, each for its own tables),
  records the last finished step and resumes there after a crash; contents are kept or deleted by
  `privacy.account_deletion_content`; afterwards `principals` and `users` hold no clear data and
  the audit chain still verifies (DP-T02). A rectification request documents the correction of an
  owner.
- Owners get `gdpr.request_due` once per open request when its deadline minus
  `privacy.request_reminder_lead` has passed (job `privacy.request_reminder`); the consumer `push`
  turns it into a hint of kind `admin`. The job `retention.privacy_job` removes finished requests
  after `retention.privacy_job`.
- Configuration keys `privacy.export_ttl`, `privacy.export_requires_approval`,
  `privacy.account_deletion_content`, `privacy.request_reminder_lead` and
  `retention.privacy_job`; migration 00079 adds `gdpr_requests` and the delete rights of the
  account deletion.
