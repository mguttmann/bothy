### Added

- Load tests of the agent path (M2-36): `make perf` measures `perf.invocation_delivery_p99`
  (`perf/invocation`, commit of a triggering message to the receipt of `agent.invocation` at the
  fake adapter) and `perf.run_patch_fanout_p99` (`perf/run_patch`, scheduled `run.update` to the
  first `run.updated` with that version at every focused viewer) under
  `perf.sustained_messages_per_s` with the default rate limits on a durable database, and fails
  when one exceeds its threshold or when the path misbehaves (a lost, duplicated or redelivered
  invocation, a rejected token, a run not `completed`, a gap or late snapshot, an error frame, a
  non-`201` answer). New packages `perf/internal/agentpath` (plan and samples),
  `perf/internal/fakeadapter` (Go fake adapter without SDK) and `perf/internal/agentworld`
  (seeding and connections); both targets join `report.Required`.
