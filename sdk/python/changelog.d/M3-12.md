### Added

- `bothy_sdk.uploads.upload_part` and `content_digest`: one part of an upload in mode `direct`
  with the server's part headers and `Content-Digest` through `Client.call_as` (M3-12).
- `Outbox.send_message(attachment_file_ids=...)`; a reply with attachments refused with `403`
  fails at once, and a failed send carries the refusal in `OutboxEntry.problem` (not stored).
- FakeServer: `getInstance` with configurable `InstanceLimits`, `createUpload`, `uploadPart`,
  `getUpload`, `completeUpload` and `abortUpload` after M3-03, `createMessage` with
  `attachment_file_ids` after M3-07, the raw body of `uploadPart` in `RecordedRequest.content`,
  and the faults `Fault.passthrough` and `Fault.hold` with `HoldGate`.
