### Added

- Module `bothy_sdk.webhooks` (M2-50, docs/spec/11-extensions.md 11.2), with the behaviour of the
  TypeScript module of M2-30: `verify_webhook` checks the three Standard Webhooks headers, the
  timestamp against `webhook.receiver_tolerance`, and the `v1a` (Ed25519) and `v1`
  (HMAC-SHA256, constant-time compare) entries over the raw body, and returns a
  `VerifiedWebhook` with the envelope decoded by the tolerance layer; `WebhookVerificationError`
  with one `code` per step and messages free of body, signature and secret;
  `parse_webhook_public_key` (`whpk_`) and `parse_webhook_secret` (`whsec_`) raise
  `WebhookConfigError`; `WebhookKeyCache` holds the keys of `getWebhookKeys` (fetched without a
  token) until the `max-age` of the answer and never fetches because a verification failed.
  Every signature case of `docs/api/webhook-test-vectors.json` verifies.
- `bothy_sdk.testing.sign_webhook` and `webhook_public_key` sign test deliveries the way the
  server does.
- `SdkSettings` fields `webhook_receiver_tolerance` (`webhook.receiver_tolerance`) and
  `webhook_keys_max_age` (`agent.jwks_max_age`).
