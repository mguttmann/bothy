### Added

- The example adapter of the guide "Connect your own agent" in `examples/guide/` (M2-37), with
  its FakeServer tests (AG-08, AG-09, AG-11, AG-12, AG-28 on the adapter side); lint, types,
  bandit, tests and coverage cover it like `src/`.
- `AgentHandler.on_commands_reconciled(result)`, called after each successful reconciliation of
  the declared commands, and `AgentHandler.on_autocomplete_sent(request, choices)`, called after
  the choices of `on_autocomplete` were sent (M2-37).
- `RuntimeStatus.reported_billing_mode` and `RuntimeStatus.billing_evidence`: the heartbeat
  reports the billing mode the runtime uses and the source of its key (M2-37).

### Fixed

- `expectation_for` compares the `command_id` of an `agent.invocation` with `trigger_kind =
  command` (M2-16 added it to the payload); before, every model command was refused with
  `invalid_invocation_token` (found by the guide's check run, M2-37).
- The FakeServer's `getMessage` shows the components of a message as they are now (state and
  version after a click or `updateComponent`), not as they were created (M2-37).
