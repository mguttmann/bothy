### Added

- Hand layer (M2-29, ADR 0026): `Adapter` and `AgentHandler` (acceptance after the token check
  and the durable inbox, start from the inbox at most once with the newest token, renewal,
  resume, cancel, refusals with the reasons of 09 4.5), `InvocationVerifier` (points 1 to 10 of
  09 4.5 in the precedence of the reference verifier, JWKS cache, `jti` store, allow list),
  SQLite inbox and outbox (mode `0600`), `Gateway` and `PollingSource` (close-code reactions,
  heartbeat, reconnect with full jitter), `Client` and `SyncClient` (DPoP proofs with nonces,
  retries only for idempotent requests, automatic `Idempotency-Key`, `Invocation-Token` inside a
  run, typed problem errors, deprecation warning), `RunPatcher` (throttled, coalesced, rebased
  patches), `render_context` with trust marks, components, commands with reconciliation and
  autocomplete guard, heartbeat builder; new key `sdk.poll_interval`.
- `bothy_sdk.testing`: FakeServer with faults, scenario functions AG-08, AG-09, AG-12, AG-28 and
  AZ-P6 run against it and against the real server, doubles and recorded fixtures.
- Runtime dependencies `PyJWT`, `cryptography`, `websockets`; extra `testing` with `aiohttp`.
- The state directory of the inbox and outbox must not be writable by group or others (POSIX).
