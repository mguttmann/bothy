### Added

- Generated types (M4-44): `Heartbeat.project_folders`, `ProjectFolderName`, `ProjectFolder`,
  `ProjectFolderList` and the operation `listAgentProjectFolders`.
- Project folders in the heartbeat (M4-44): `buildHeartbeat(state, { projectFolders })` sends the
  folder names a runner finds under its projects root (distinct, one segment of letters, digits,
  `.`, `_` and `-` that starts with a letter or digit, at most 255 characters); leaving it out
  means unchanged.
