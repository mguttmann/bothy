### Added

- Long answers stay one message (M4-47): `sendLongMessage` sends an answer within
  `message_max_content_bytes` as it is and a longer one as one message with the head and the rest
  as one attachment `answer.txt` (`splitForLimit`, `uploadText`); when that upload fails the head
  goes out with a short note. `Outbox.sendMessage` takes `attachmentFileIds`.
