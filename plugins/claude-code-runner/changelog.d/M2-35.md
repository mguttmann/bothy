### Added

- Follow-ups over stdin (M2-35): `capabilities.followups = steer`; a follow-up is read with
  `getMessage`, checked again at the moment of writing, written as one `user` line with the
  lead-in `Follow-up from <name> (@<handle>):` and its own `uuid`, patched `delivered` and, on
  the echo, `absorbed`; follow-ups of a run that has not started are held, those without echo are
  written again into the next process of a recovery; stdin stays open for
  `runner.followup_grace` after a `result` while follow-ups lack their echo.
- Stop (M2-35): `run.cancel_requested` sends SIGINT to the turn's group and closes stdin, then
  SIGTERM after `runner.stop_grace` and SIGKILL after `runner.kill_grace` (log code
  `stop_escalated`), and finishes `cancelled` with the request's reason once no process of the
  group is left; a run the server ended has its turn stopped without a finish.
- Error paths of docs/spec/09-agents.md 18.9 (M2-35): the table `ERROR_TEXTS` of the pinned CLI's
  texts and the classification of every process end; one compaction with `/compact` and a retry
  with a hint in the runner prompt; a new session for a missing one (fail open, with
  `bind_failed` in mode `bind`); `--resume` for a session that exists at the first turn;
  `runtime_not_authenticated` at a process end; the error texts of a `result` in the box note
  `runtime_error`; box notes `context_compacted` and `session_missing`.
- Adoption after a crash of the runner (M2-35): turn records with the process identity, the
  stream offset, answered results and the follow-up map; at start the runner adopts, stops,
  finishes from the log or leaves each turn, signals only a process whose identity still
  matches, and lists adopted runs in `active_runs`.
- Recovery invariants (M2-35, D-M2-35-28): the turn ledger keeps the stored offset at the run's
  end evidence until the run is finished, stores each reply's `client_msg_id` before the send,
  answers an input by its own `uuid` (a success `result` that names it, or its echo before a
  success `result`) and keeps a process's kind; own and adopted turns read their stream through
  one loop, and an adopted turn patches `absorbed` on the echoes it reads. A crash-point test
  enumerates every first and second crash point over the recovery scenarios.
- Fake-Claude: a session store, full contexts, `/compact`, API error texts, one `result` per input
  line, echoes when a turn takes the line (like the pinned CLI), on read, after the result or
  never, follow-ups folded into a notification turn, a stderr canary and more gates; scenarios of
  every row of 18.9, follow-ups, stop and privacy; the switch test `test_pinned_cli.py`.
