### Added

- Permission hints and "Expiring soon" (M6-07, 07 sections 9.4 and 9.5): two lists in the
  administration of the desktop app and of the console (under "Groups, grants"). "Permission
  hints" shows, in the reader's language, who can learn content of a channel through an agent
  (H1), grants to disabled principals, `trigger` grants that cannot trigger (tool class or dev
  isolation) and agents in personal mode with their trigger set, with "Explain" (who has access)
  and, for grants, "Revoke" and "Extend". "Expiring soon" lists grants, group memberships and
  agent and app tokens that expire within `authz.expiry_reminder_lead`; "Extend" opens the grant
  wizard with the old grant and a new reason and creates the new grant before it revokes the old
  one, with one step-up; an agent token is renewed in the agent's token screen.
