### Added

- Autonomous runs in the desktop and phone apps (AR-08, 01 6.21): the pending wake-ups of a
  thread above the composer with their condition, earliest fire, deadline and Cancel for the
  wake-up's person and a person with `manage`; the end state of a finished run on its line and
  when the thread continues; "Woke up: <label>" at a wake run and "Continued after lost work" at
  a recovery run; the notes of wake-up ends, caps, a waiting end without a wake-up and the usage
  limit as translated notes of the thread; and the channel switch "Let agents continue on their
  own" in the channel menu. The local database (schema version 14) keeps the wake-ups of a
  thread, and the sync engine applies `run.wake_changed`.
- `RunMetrics` of the API names `end_state` and `wake_pending_until` of 03 `runs.metrics`.
