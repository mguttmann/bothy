### Added

- Long pastes become a file (M4-47, 01 6.13, R-PR-26): in the composer of a thread and of a new
  post, a paste of more than `ui.composer_paste_file_chars` (4000) characters or
  `ui.composer_paste_file_lines` (100) lines becomes the attachment `paste.txt`, or
  `paste.<extension>` for one fenced code block or a recognizable file, shown as a chip with
  "Insert as text".
- Long texts stay one message (M4-47): a text larger than `message.max_content_bytes` shows a note
  and goes out as one message whose rest is the attachment `message.txt`, instead of the limit
  error; the new chat view of a forum sends attachments with its first message.
