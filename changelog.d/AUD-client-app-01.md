### Added

- The column borders of the desktop app can be moved without a mouse: Tab reaches a border, the
  left and right arrow keys move it, and a screen reader changes the width of the column through
  it as a slider.

### Fixed

- The phone apps show a notification that arrives while the app is in front: a banner at the top
  of the screen with the channel, the thread and the text, which opens the thread at the message
  on a tap. Until now a mention, a question of a run or the hint about a new device only moved a
  counter while the app was open, and no push followed either.
- The app lock of the phones no longer closes while a person uses the app with a screen reader
  or a switch control: what they do through VoiceOver, TalkBack or switch control counts as use,
  like a touch or a key.
- The phone and Mac apps ask for the notification permission also when the first thing a person
  opens is a text channel. Until now only a thread or a forum post asked, so a person who read
  only text channels never got a push.
