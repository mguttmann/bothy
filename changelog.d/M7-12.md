### Added

- Desktop app distribution (M7-12, docs/spec/18-release.md 8.1, ADR 0037): the template
  `apps/distribution/distribution.yaml` with its JSON Schema and the appcast, `.appinstaller` and
  MSIX manifest templates; `make -C apps/client release-apple` builds the macOS app with Sparkle 2
  (Developer ID signature, notarization, `spctl`, EdDSA-signed archive and appcast in the channel
  beta with a phased rollout) and `release-windows` the signed MSIX with `beta.appinstaller`; the
  release jobs `build-apple` and `build-windows` sign only with the secrets of their environments
  and stop before signing without them. `tools/distribution/appdist.py` promotes a build from
  beta to stable without a rebuild and merges a release into the published appcast. The macOS app
  starts Sparkle's updater when the build carries it (`AppUpdater`). `make -C apps/client
  trial-apple` and `trial-windows` prove the chain with a test publisher, including an update of
  an installed older build through Sparkle and App Installer; CI runs them in the jobs
  `desktop trial (macOS)` and `desktop trial (Windows)` for changes of the release steps.
