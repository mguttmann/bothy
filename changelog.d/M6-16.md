### Added

- Live views in the apps and the console (M6-16, 01 5.1 D12 and D13, 5.3 M10, 5.5; 25 6, 7.1,
  9.1): the six views of `bothy_viz` run with real data in all three hosts through the new page
  `LiveScreen` of `admin_ui` (one tab per view with its table view, a scope selector of all
  spaces or one space, TV mode over the whole window on the desktop and in the console, "Open in
  Grafana" only when `telemetry_dashboard_url` is delivered, in the system browser or a new tab).
  The desktop app has the sidebar group "Live and statistics" with "Live" (`/live`) and
  "Statistics"; the phone has "Live" under Me (M10, no TV mode, a run card opens its thread); the
  console fills its page "Live" (a run card says "Open in the app").
- `sync_engine` gains `StatsSubscription` (`SyncEngine.stats`): `stats.subscribe` once when live,
  no resend after `resumed`, a resend after `identify`, an `error` of `stats.subscribe` clears it
  and passes the code; `stats.live` frames go to a stream and never to the local database. The
  apps feed the views through `EngineLiveFrameSource`; on the phone the stream runs only in the
  foreground.
- The console polls `getStatsLive` every `viz.live_poll_interval` (`PollingLiveFrameSource`),
  stops while the tab is hidden (Page Visibility), ends on `feature_disabled`, `forbidden` or
  `not_found`, and backs off up to `viz.stale_after` on other failures.
- `ApiStatsQuerySource` in `admin_ui`: the query operations of the views over the client of each
  host (DPoP in the apps, the cookie client in the console).
- VZ-T16 in `make arch` (`tools/arch-check/no_webview.py`): no WebView package, no
  `HtmlElementView` or iframe and no native WebView in `apps/client`.
- The fake server of M4-17 serves `stats.subscribe` and `stats.unsubscribe` (the subscription
  lives in the gateway session, so `resume` restores it), sends `stats.live` when a test asks
  (`GatewayFake.sendStatsLive`) and answers `getStatsLive`, `getStatsTimeline`, `getStatsSlo` and
  `getStatsBackends` with stored bodies (the fixtures of `bothy_viz`).
