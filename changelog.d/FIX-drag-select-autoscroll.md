### Fixed

- Selecting with the mouse on macOS and Windows no longer stops at the edge of what is on
  screen. In the chat history a drag continues across messages, and holding the pointer above
  or below the history scrolls it while the selection grows; the copy contains every selected
  message in order. Shift and a click extend a selection. In the terminal the same drag scrolls
  through the scrollback, faster the farther the pointer is out, and the copy keeps every line.
