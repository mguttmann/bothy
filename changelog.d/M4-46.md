### Changed

- Sidebar forum groups list every open post (M4-46, 01 6.8, R-PR-23): under each forum the
  sidebar shows all open posts, newest activity first, instead of only the unread, running or
  recently active ones; done posts leave the group. Beyond `ui.sidebar_forum_posts_visible` (15)
  the row "Show more" above "All posts" lists the rest in place and "Show less" goes back; the
  choice is kept on this device (`local_store` schema version 9,
  `channel_view_states.posts_shown_in_full`). While the pointer or focus is in the sidebar the
  rows keep their order. Text channels keep their rule; `ui.sidebar_thread_window` and
  `ui.sidebar_threads_per_channel` now apply to them only.
