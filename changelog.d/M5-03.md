### Added

- Android app (M5-03): the Android runner (API 26 and later) with its application ID, label, URL
  scheme and App Links host from the build branding (`application_id` and `associated_domain`,
  neutral `example.com` defaults); the phone screens of the iPhone with Android controls: Material
  pages, the top app bar with the up arrow, and system back that pops the stack, closes a sheet
  and leaves the app on a tab's first page; the notification channels Mentions, Questions, All
  messages and Runs with localized names, registered at every start, and the mapping of the
  notification kinds to them; login in a Custom Tab, passkeys through the Credential Manager
  only with an App Links host (API 34 and later); no backup or device transfer of the app's data;
  the gateway closes in the background, and the recent apps screen shows no content. The phone
  integration tests and the instrumented tests of the runner run on an emulator in
  `make apps-platform` and the `apps-android-26` and `apps-android-36` jobs.
