### Added

- The live topology shows the agent tree of each run below its card: subagents, workflows with
  their agents and background jobs, each with its role, state, runtime and current tool, joined by

  leave after a short time, and the table lists the children indented below their run. The same
  tree appears on desktop, phone and in the web console (25 6.1).
