### Changed

- Several photos from the library at once on the phone (M5-16, 15 14): "Photo library" in the
  attachment menu of the iOS and Android composer now picks several photos in one go, up to the
  free slots of `message_max_attachments`; they become the attachments of one message in the
  picker's order, all of them or none with the reason. With every slot taken the picker does not
  open and the composer says why. The camera still takes one photo. The attachment chips scroll
  within a quarter of the screen, so the input stays visible with ten attachments.
