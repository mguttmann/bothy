### Added

- Pinning channels and posts in the own sidebar (FEAT-PIN-CHANNELS, 01 6.24, R-PR-39): on macOS,
  Windows, iOS and Android the menu of a channel or forum (right click, the menu key or Shift+F10
  on the desktop, a long press on the phone, and the More menu of its header) has "Pin to top" and,
  while pinned, "Unpin"; the menu of a forum post has the same entry. A pinned channel leads its
  space and a pinned post leads its forum, with a small pin, oldest pin first, before the manual
  order and before "Arrange channels and projects automatically", which stays unchanged for the
  rest. The pin is personal and follows the person to every device.
