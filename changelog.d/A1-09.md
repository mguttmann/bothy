- Built-in agents can be edited on the desktop, the phone and the web console: the agent card
  (on the phone "Built-in agents" under Me) offers "Edit" with the preview of "New agent", the
  changed parts named before "Save" and the class steps in the order of 26 8.4; "Versions" with
  "Show" and "Restore" (an older version saved again as a new one); "Change runner" with the
  server's refusals as sentences; "Disable" with a reason; and "Continue setup" for an agent
  that is "Not set up". A change of another device answers with "Reload" (A1-09).

  `getAgentDefinition` with `version`, `setAgentHost` with its refusals and `disableAgent`
  (A1-09).
