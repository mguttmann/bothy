### Added

- Windows notification area and local notifications (M4-30): closing the window hides the app to
  the notification area, where it keeps its gateway connection; the tray icon has Open and Quit,
  and Quit ends the connection. Local notifications come only from `notification.created`, on
  Windows and on macOS while connected: title and text from the preview fields or the placeholder
  with the local channel name, quiet without sound, one slot per thread, nothing for the thread
  open in the focused window, a click opens the thread at the message. The app reports `idle`
  while its window is hidden, minimized or in the background (15 10). Settings > Notifications
  says that a closed app gets no notifications and holds "Start at login", a device setting that
  is off by default. The sync engine exposes `SyncEngine.notifications`, and the fake server
  decides and delivers hints with `FakeWorld.notify`.
