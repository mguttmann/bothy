### Added

- Search assistant in the apps (macOS, Windows, iPhone, Android; 14 4.10, 01 D6a): the quick
  switcher (Cmd+K or Ctrl+K) shows "Ask the search assistant" for any typed text (also Cmd+Enter
  or Ctrl+Enter), and the search view has the same button, which the phone reaches from the lens
  at the top. A short-lived popup takes the question in your own words and lists the matching
  chat and development sessions with place, time and one sentence why, or says that the hits are
  full text; follow-up questions refine it, a tap opens the thread at the matching message, and
  each answer shows the assistant's cost estimate for information. Closing the popup forgets the
  conversation.
