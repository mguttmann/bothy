### Added

- Sidebar ordering (M4-51, 01 6.15, R-PR-29): on macOS, Windows, iOS and Android the spaces, the
  channels and forums of a space and the open posts of a forum can be put into an order of one's
  own. On the desktop a row is dragged with the mouse and a line shows where it lands; on the
  phone a long press lifts the row. The sidebar menus of spaces, channels and posts have "Move up"
  and "Move down", and screen readers have the same actions. The order follows the person to
  every device and never changes what others see. Three switches in Settings, Appearance arrange
  spaces, channels or chats by their last activity instead (chats are on by default, as before);
  an automatically arranged level cannot be dragged and says so.
