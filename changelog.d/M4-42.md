### Added

- Desktop thread layout (M4-42, 01 D1, D2, R-PR-19): when a thread or a forum post is open next
  to its channel (the main history of a text channel or the pinned forum overview), "Hide
  channel" in the thread header, Cmd+Shift+H (Ctrl+Shift+H) and the Thread menu hide that column
  so the thread takes the whole content width; "Show channel" brings it back. The choice is kept
  on this device (`local_store` schema version 7, `shell_view_state.channel_column_hidden`) and
  stays when another thread opens from the sidebar.
- The border between the main history of a text channel and the thread beside it can be
  dragged; its width is stored as `main_history` in `layout.column_widths`, and neither side
  becomes narrower than the column minimum.

### Fixed

- Header actions of a thread are cut with an ellipsis instead of overflowing when the column is
  at its minimum width (M4-42).
