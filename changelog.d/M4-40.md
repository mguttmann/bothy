### Added

- Sessions in the desktop sidebar (M4-40, 01 6.8, D-SB-1 to D-SB-10): under each forum and text
  channel the sidebar shows its open threads as nested rows with a tree line, chosen by the rules
  of 01 6.8 (selected, active run, waiting for me, unread, recent activity; at most
  `ui.sidebar_threads_per_channel` with a fixed priority), ordered by creation and never moved
  under the pointer; each row carries its run state icon, unread mark and mention counter, an
  untitled post reads "New chat" with the "automatic" mark, and an expanded forum group ends with
  "All posts", which carries the counters of the posts not shown. Each group collapses with its
  own arrow; the state is kept on this device. The arrow keys walk the tree, and "next or previous
  channel" and "next unread" stop at thread rows too.
- Forum overview D15 (M4-40, R-PR-17): a click on a forum opens its overview (post list, kind
  sentence, "New chat" or "New post", search in the forum, filters, Done section) in the content
  column; "Pin" or Cmd+Shift+O (Ctrl+Shift+O) keeps it as its own column, kept on this device,
  and below `ui.desktop_min_width_three_columns` it returns to the content column.
- Channel kinds explained (M4-40, D-SB-10): text channel, forum and project forum have their own
  icon (new `channel.project`, Phosphor `brackets-angle`), a tooltip and screen reader text with
  the kind sentence, the sentence as the subtitle of the overview and as the empty state of a
  forum.
- Sessions in the sidebar, data part (M4-40, 04 6.3 item 4, R-SY-28, R-DM-54): the sync engine
  stores the snapshot's `active_threads` of the held channels on the first connection and on
  every resync, loads a thread it does not hold with one `getThread` when a message of it
  arrives, and keeps `Channel.project_bound` from the snapshot, `getChannel` and
  `channel.updated`. The local database moves to schema version 6: the column
  `channels.project_bound` and the local-only tables `channel_view_states` (collapsed thread
  group per channel) and `shell_view_state` (pinned forum overview), kept per device and never
  synchronized; the migration clears the cursor, so the next connection loads a whole snapshot.
  The fake server of `sync_engine` binds a project with `FakeWorld.setProjectBound`.
