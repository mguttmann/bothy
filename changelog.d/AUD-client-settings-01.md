### Added

- Settings, Privacy: "Export of your data" requests the export of your own data, shows where the
  request stands and saves the archive of a completed export on the desktop; a phone shows the
  state and names the desktop app for the download. Requesting and downloading ask for a
  confirmation (step-up).

### Fixed

- The apps keep your language and time zone when they start without a connection to the
  instance, instead of falling back to the system language and to UTC, and ask the instance
  again when the connection is back.
- A setting the instance refuses to store is no longer shown as if it were saved: the settings
  name it with the reason and offer "Send again" and "Discard", and discarding shows the stored
  value again.
