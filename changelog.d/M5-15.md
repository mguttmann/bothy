### Added

- Threads in the sidebar and new chat on iPhone and Android (M5-15, 01 6.8, D-SB-13): M1 Spaces
  shows the open posts and threads under their channel with the same selection, bound, order,
  "All posts", markers and counters as the desktop sidebar, and "+" on every forum row at the
  touch target size. A tap on a thread row opens its history, a tap on the forum name or "All
  posts" the post list, and "+" or the draft row opens the new chat view as a page with the input
  focused; back leaves it and deletes an empty draft, and sending creates the post through the
  outbox and opens its history. A row that no longer qualifies stays while a finger is on the
  list, the list scrolls or a screen reader is on M1. The collapse state of a group is kept per
  device; the phone has no pinned column.

### Changed

- Sidebar (M5-15): a channel with thread rows and its rows are nested nodes of the semantics
  tree, so screen readers get the levels space, channel, thread on the desktop too.
