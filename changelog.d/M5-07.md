### Added

- Voice messages and photos on the phone (M5-07): press and hold the microphone to record a voice
  message (AAC-LC in `.m4a`, mono, `client.voice.bitrate`), swipe left to discard, swipe up to
  lock, release to send; the remaining time shows, a notice comes `client.voice.limit_notice_lead`
  before `voice_max_duration_ms`, and a recording that reaches the limit stops by itself shortly
  before it, so the duration the server reads never exceeds the limit. With VoiceOver,
  TalkBack or Switch Control on, the microphone records on a tap and stops on a second tap, and
  the remaining time is a live region announced at most once per
  `a11y.live_announce_min_interval`. The background or a call stops a running recording and keeps
  it for the person. The attachment button opens camera, photo library or file; photos are
  uploaded as JPEG through the outbox with a local preview. Microphone and camera are asked for
  at their first use with texts from `packages/l10n` (iOS `InfoPlist.strings` per language,
  generated and checked by `make codegen-check`); a denied permission explains the way to the
  settings. New configuration key `client.voice.limit_notice_lead`.

### Changed

- A voice recording of the desktop also stops the same margin before `voice_max_duration_ms`, and
  its duration is the `mvhd` duration of the file, the value the server checks (M5-07).
