### Added

- Archiving a channel or project for oneself (PROJ-ARCHIVE, 01 6.22, R-PR-38): on macOS, Windows,
  iOS and Android the sidebar menu of a channel or forum (right click, the menu key or Shift+F10
  on the desktop, a long press on the phone) has "Archive project" for a project forum and
  "Archive" for every other channel, with the same screen reader action. The channel leaves the
  normal list and shows in the collapsed section "Archived (n)" at the bottom of its space;
  "Restore" in its menu brings it back to its earlier place. Nothing is deleted or made read only,
  no right changes, runs keep going, its unread items and mentions still show, and the archive
  follows the person to every device. Whether the section is expanded is kept per device.
