### Added

- Host service release (M7-23): `release.yml` builds the host binaries and Linux packages in
  `build`, signs and notarizes the `.pkg` in `build-apple` and signs the MSI in `build-windows`
  for tags `host/v<version>`, and the new job `host-signing` writes the SBOMs, signatures and
  `SHA256SUMS` of all of them. `make release-trial COMPONENT=host` proves the binaries and the
  Linux packages (installed in throwaway Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora and AlmaLinux containers); the CI
  jobs `host trial (macOS)` and `host trial (Windows)` build, sign and install the `.pkg` and the
  MSI with a test publisher when the host service or its release steps change. nfpm 2.48.0 and the
  WiX Toolset 5.0.2 are pinned build tools.
