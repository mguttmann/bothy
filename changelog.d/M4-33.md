### Added

- Channel menu, notification levels and channel export (M4-33): the menus of a space, a channel
  and a thread (right click, the menu key or Shift+F10 on a sidebar row or a post, the "More"
  button of the thread header) set the level All, Mentions or Mute, mute for an hour, until
  tomorrow 08:00 in the person's time zone or until a chosen time, or inherit again, and show
  the inherited level with its source and "Following" for a followed thread (13 3.1, 3.2).
  Settings > Notifications shows D9: the personal default, the content of notifications, the
  quiet hours rules with weekdays, times, "All day", time zone and switch, "Questions break
  quiet hours", the administration reminders for owners and the tree of levels with visible
  inheritance. Every change goes through the outbox and reaches the person's other devices.
  "Export history" appears only with the capability `export`, asks for the step-up the server
  demands and streams the chosen range unchanged into a file with a byte progress; cancel or a
  broken stream deletes the partial file.
- Fake server (sync engine testing): the notification preference and quiet hours operations
  with `notification_pref.updated` and `quiet_hours.updated`, automatic following on a write in
  a thread (13 3.2) and `exportChannel` with capability, step-up and range.

### Fixed

- The sidebar marks a channel muted when its own row gives `mute` after 13 3.1: a stored `mute`
  whose `muted_until` has passed stays muted, and a temporary mute of another level counts while
  it lasts.
