### Added

- App lock on the phones (M5-08, 08 9, 01 5.6 E6): when the person turns the lock on in Settings,
  Security, or the owner requires it with `security.app_lock_required`, the app shows E6 at a
  cold start and after `client.app_lock_after` without use, in front or in the background, and
  hides every route behind it; a deep link or a tapped notification opens after the unlock.
  Unlocking uses the system's biometrics or device credential (`local_auth`); a failure keeps E6
  with "Try again", "Log out" deletes the local data and shows E7. A required lock on a device
  without a device credential shows only a notice and "Log out". The lock emits `locked` and
  `unlocked` events for the terminal view. The policy comes from `getInstance` and is cached in
  the local database (schema version 5).
