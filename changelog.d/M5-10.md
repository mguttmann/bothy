### Added

- Accessibility of the phone apps (M5-10): the checklist `docs/quality/accessibility-mobile.md`
  with every rule of 01 section 8 for every phone screen of M5 on iOS with VoiceOver and on
  Android with TalkBack, the manual pass before a release and a test that every page of the
  phone router has a row; per screen tests of a shared harness (`dsPhoneAccessibilityTests` in
  `package:design_system/testing.dart`: screen reader audit with a semantics snapshot, contrast,
  the touch targets of iOS and Android, text scale 2, reduced motion, the language of the
  semantics tree); the audit of Xcode (`performAccessibilityAudit`) on the iOS simulator and the
  checks of the Accessibility Test Framework on Android, both in the platform tests.

### Fixed

- Phone accessibility gaps the new tests found: a banner with an action kept its note readable
  at large text, the badge of the Running tab and the share preview say what they are, the run
  graph toggle and the space groups of the sidebar have names, and empty gaps of the share sheet,
  the emergency pages and the Me page are no stops for screen readers.
