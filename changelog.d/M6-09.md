### Added

- Instance administration in the desktop app and the web console (M6-09, 01 5.5 "Instance,
  branding"): owners see every instance setting of the configuration reference with its type,
  allowed values, value, version and who changed it, and change it with a typed editor; manage
  the branding (product name with the server's checks shown at the field, the logo with the
  server's preview, the accent from the seven palette keys only, with a preview of its roles in
  light and dark); add, change, disable and delete OIDC, proxy header and local account login
  providers (the redirect URI to register with a copy action, secret references written only,
  the loopback rule of the proxy header driver, and a warning that the provider's sessions end);
  and switch feature flags with a reason, seeing the state evaluated for themselves. Every change
  goes through the step-up, states what it changes before the confirmation and detects a
  concurrent change by another owner (the newer value is shown and the person is asked again).
  The console reads its branding again after a change, so its theme follows the new accent.
  `make console-e2e` runs the `patrol` web flows of a setting, the accent, a flag and a new
  login provider.
