### Added

- API contract of the wake-ups (AR-04): the wake-up operations, `setChannelAutonomousRuns`, the
  member `allow_autonomous_runs` of `Channel`, `triggering_wake_id` and the trigger kind `wake` of
  `Run`, the notification kind `wake_ended`, the heartbeat capabilities `turn_handover` and
  `wakes`, and the durable events `run.wake_changed` and `agent.wake_input`; the Dart client and
  the event catalogue know them, and the sync engine skips the events until the client views come.
