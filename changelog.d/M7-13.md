### Added

- Phone app distribution and the minimum app version (M7-13, docs/spec/18-release.md 8.2 and 9,
  docs/spec/15-client-architecture.md 7.2 and 15): `make -C apps/client release-ios` builds the
  iOS app with the publisher values of `distribution.yaml`, signs it for the App Store, uploads it
  to App Store Connect and adds it to the TestFlight group beta (`promote` adds it to stable);
  `release-android` builds the App Bundle signed with the upload key, checks it with jarsigner,
  bundletool and apksigner and releases it on the internal Play track (`promote` on
  `android.stable_track`). The release job `build-apple` runs the iOS step, the new job
  `build-android` runs in the new environment `release-android`; both stop before anything is
  signed without their secrets. `trial-ios` (unsigned archive, identifiers, versions and
  entitlements) and `trial-android` (throwaway upload key, signature and structure checks) prove
  the chain without an account; CI runs them in `mobile trial (iOS)` and `mobile trial (Android)`.
  `distribution.yaml` has the new keys `ios.update_url`, `android.update_url` and
  `android.stable_track`.
- The apps enforce `client.min_version.<platform>` and `client.recommended_version.<platform>`
  of `getDiscovery` with SemVer precedence: below the minimum an app opens no gateway connection,
  also when the instance raises it while the app runs (checked before every reconnect), keeps its
  outbox and local data and offers the update path of its build (TestFlight or the store,
  Sparkle, the `.appinstaller` file); below the recommended version it shows a notice the person
  dismisses once per version; `4007` shows "Update required" with the update path.
