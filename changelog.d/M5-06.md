### Added

- Sharing from other apps (M5-06): on iOS and macOS a Share Extension, Swift and SwiftUI without
  the Flutter engine, shows its own sheet (preview, comment, the recently used targets with write
  permission, a search over thread titles and channels) and sends by itself: the files are
  uploaded with the upload protocol streamed from disk (photos as JPEG), then the message or a
  new forum post is created with the extension's own `client_msg_id`. It signs in with a child
  session of its own (`createExtensionSession`, `client_component = share_ext`) that the app puts
  into the shared keychain and that only the extension refreshes. Without network or without a
  valid child session the share goes into the share inbox of the App Group, and the app takes it
  over into its outbox at the next start with the same `client_msg_id`, exactly once. On Android
  a share activity takes `ACTION_SEND` and `ACTION_SEND_MULTIPLE` for an explicit list of
  concrete types and opens the same target sheet in the app, which sends through the outbox. The
  app keeps the recent targets in the App Group and deletes them with the account. Device tests
  with `patrol` share an image from Photos and from the file app into a thread, and ID-T16 runs
  with the real Share Extension against the Compose stack (`tools/e2e/id_t16.sh`).
