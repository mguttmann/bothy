### Changed

- Readable code blocks (M4-49, 01 6.14, R-PR-28): on macOS, Windows, iOS and Android a code
  block now wraps its long lines by default, so its whole content is visible before copying, and
  an indented line keeps its indentation when it wraps. The toggle "Wrap lines" in the block
  header switches every code block of the device to one line per line with a horizontal scroll
  that works: a visible scrollbar on the desktop, shift and the mouse wheel, the trackpad, and a
  drag on the phone; the choice is kept on the device. A block higher than
  `ui.code_block_collapse_lines` (20) lines shows collapsed with a fade and "Show all", and
  "Show less" collapses it again. Copying a block still copies its exact text.
