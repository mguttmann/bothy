### Added

- Status page in the apps and the console (M7-21, 16 12): `admin_ui` gains `StatusScreen` with
  the overall state, the components with their states (word, icon and state role), the open
  incidents, the maintenance windows, the history with the owner texts in the safe Markdown
  subset of messages, and the maintenance mode shown apart from outages. The desktop app opens it
  at `/status` from the foot of the sidebar, the iPhone and Android apps from the Me tab; the
  apps reload it on `status.updated` (`SyncEngine.statusUpdates`) and show "Server unreachable"
  and no state while the connection state reports the server unreachable. The console hosts it
  on `/console/status` and reads `getStatus` every `client.status_poll_interval` (new App key,
  default `30s`). Owners maintain components, incidents, maintenance windows and their updates
  in the administration (`/admin/status`, `/console/status`) with forms that send only the
  fields of the seven operations. The console serves the public status page at
  `/console/public-status` (linked from the login), which shows the page only with
  `status.public_enabled`. The fake server of M4-17 serves the seven status operations and
  appends `status.updated`.
