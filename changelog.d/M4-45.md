### Added

- Desktop session column toggle (M4-45, 01 D1, D2, R-PR-22): the header button of a thread or
  main history reads "Hide session" or "Show session" instead of "Column"; with Cmd+Shift+I
  (Ctrl+Shift+I), the Thread menu and "Pin" it hides and shows the right session column, and a
  slim rail at the right edge brings a hidden column back. The choice is kept on this device per
  layout (`local_store` schema version 8, `shell_view_state.session_column_hidden` and
  `text_thread_session_shown`) and stays when another thread opens or the app restarts.
