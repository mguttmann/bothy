### Added

- Terminal on the phone (M5-12): screen M9 lists the hosts with their connection, the LLM login
  badge and the person's own sessions, and screen M8 shows a session full screen with a session
  switcher, "Detach" and "End" and the extra keys bar (Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, the arrows, `|`, `~`,
  `/`, `-`). Ctrl and Alt act once on a tap and stay latched after a second tap; a hardware
  keyboard hides the bar. A long press selects text for "Copy", "Paste" asks first when the text
  has more than one line, and two fingers scroll the scrollback. The background, the app lock and
  system back detach the session, and the app attaches it again after the lock; Android keeps
  the terminal out of screenshots and the recent apps, iOS hides it while the screen is recorded,
  and the soft keyboard learns nothing from terminal input.
