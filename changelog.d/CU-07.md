- Computer use in the apps (CU-07, 27 11): while an agent controls one of the owner's desktops,
  the thread of its run shows a status chip on macOS, Windows, iOS and Android with the desktop,
  the frontmost app and the action count, or that the screen is locked, and a Stop button; after
  the end the chip names the reason once. The host page of the console and the desktop app and
  the hosts screen of the phone show each desktop's enablement, account and permissions with
  "Switch enablement off" (switching on stays at the machine). The tray of the desktop app has
  "Stop computer control" while a session is live (on macOS a menu bar icon appears only then).
  Owners only; no view shows a screenshot.
