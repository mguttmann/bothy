### Added

- Forums for existing folders on the desktop (M4-44, 01 6.10, R-PR-21): for an owner the space
  menu has "Add forums for existing folders". The dialog lists the folders the chosen runner
  reported under its projects folder that have no forum yet, each with a checked box, and "Add
  forums" creates a forum named like each checked folder, assigns the runner when needed and
  binds the folder, one folder after the other, then waits until the runner reports them ready.
  Every folder shows its state and, on a failure, the sentence of the new project dialog; "Try
  again" resumes the failed folders without creating a second forum. A folder that already has a
  forum is never offered.
- Rename project (M4-44, 01 6.11): the menu of a forum with a project binding has "Rename
  project" for a person who may manage it; it renames the forum, while the folder on the runner
  and the binding stay as they are.
