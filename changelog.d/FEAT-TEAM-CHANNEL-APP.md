### Added

- Team channels in the app, for owners: the channel menu of a text channel has "Team channel..."
  with the team mode switch, the members with remove buttons, a form that adds an agent with one of
  the channels it operates and a handle, and "Stop all". Every thread of a team channel shows a bar
  with each member's state (idle, working, paused), why the channel is paused and "Stop all". A
  member's reply in the team channel says which member wrote it, an owner's message says that it
  reaches every member, and the composer offers `/team list`, `/team add`, `/team remove @handle`
  and `/team stop`. German and English.
