### Added

- Terminal E2E on the phones (M5-13): `make e2e-mobile` also runs a terminal suite on the iOS
  simulator and the Android emulator against a real server with a real host service in a
  container, paired through the API. It opens a shell from the hosts screen with the step-up,
  ends a running command with Ctrl+C from the extra keys bar, reattaches after a network loss
  with the scrollback replayed, checks that the app switcher shows no terminal content, and
  reattaches without a step-up within `terminal.quick_reattach_window` and with one after it.
  The accessibility checklist of the phones now covers the terminal and the hosts screen.

### Fixed

- Phone terminal accessibility (M5-13): screen readers can press and latch the keys of the extra
  keys bar and open the session switcher (it reads as disabled with one session), "New session"
  in M9 and M8 is a control of its own with a full touch target, the hosts screen reads the LLM
  login of each host, and its badge wraps below the name at large text sizes instead of
  overflowing.
