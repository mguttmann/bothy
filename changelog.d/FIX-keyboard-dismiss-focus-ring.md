### Fixed

- On iPhone and Android a tap outside a text field (the message list, empty space, the header)
  closes the keyboard, and dragging the message list closes it too; the draft stays. Attach,
  microphone, send and the suggestions of the composer work with the keyboard open. This holds
  for every screen and dialog of the app.
- A focused text field no longer shows a thick yellow ring: it shows the caret and a thin
  outline in the theme's accent, in light and dark. Buttons and other controls keep a visible
  keyboard focus ring, now in the theme's accent instead of yellow, also in the sidebar.
