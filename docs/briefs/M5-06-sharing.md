# M5-06: Sharing from other apps

Status: ready | Depends on: M1-73, M4-22, M5-03, M5-04 | Milestone: M5

## Goal

After this ticket a person can share text, links, images and files from another app into a chosen thread or channel (01 5.3 M6, 15 12, ADR 0056 item 2). On iOS and macOS a Share Extension in Swift and SwiftUI, without the Flutter engine, shows its own sheet with a preview, a comment field and the target list: the recently used targets with write permission that the app keeps in the App Group container (at most `client.share.recent_targets`) and a search for more (thread titles through `searchThreadTitles`, channels from the person's structure). It sends by itself through `ClientKit`: the file is uploaded with the upload protocol of 12 streamed from disk, then the message is created with a `client_msg_id` generated by the extension (or a new forum post through `createThread` with `first_message`). The extension authenticates with its own child session (`client_component = 'share_ext'`) that the app creates with `createExtensionSession` and puts into the shared keychain group; the extension refreshes only that family, one refresh at a time (08 7.4, R-CL-07). Without network, or when the child session is missing or revoked, the extension writes the entry with its `client_msg_id` into the share inbox of the App Group; the app takes it over into its outbox at the next start, and on a duplicate the server returns the original. On Android a share activity with `ACTION_SEND` and `ACTION_SEND_MULTIPLE` for concrete MIME types opens the same target sheet in Flutter and sends through the app's outbox. A device test with `patrol` proves that an image from another app lands in the chosen thread, and ID-T16 runs with the real Share Extension: a simultaneous refresh of app and extension triggers no reuse detection. Why: sharing must not depend on the person opening the app later, and a share extension cannot open its own app (15 12).

## Design notes

1. **Extension sheet** (01 5.3 M6; 15 12; Ruling for docs/DECISIONS.md). SwiftUI: a header with Cancel and Send, a preview of the first item with name and size, a comment field, the target list (recent targets from `TargetStore`, newest first, at most `client.share.recent_targets`; a target is a thread, a channel's main timeline or "new post" in a forum channel), a search field that queries `searchThreadTitles` and filters the person's channels from `listSpaces` by name, and the note "Only targets with write permission." Send is disabled until a target is chosen. The sheet never shows message content of a target.

2. **Loading items** (15 12, 14). `NSItemProvider` items are loaded as file URLs (or data written to a file for text and images that come as data), copied into the App Group container under a fresh folder per share, coordinated reads (`NSFileCoordinator`), never read fully into memory. Photos (`public.image` from the library) are converted to JPEG by the system (`UIImage` decode is a memory risk: use `CGImageSource` with a thumbnail-free full decode to JPEG through `CGImageDestination`, streaming where possible, and record the measured peak memory for a large photo) (12 7.1, ADR 0047). Sizes over `file_max_upload_bytes` (from the instance limits written to the App Group by the app) are rejected in the sheet with a reason.

3. **Child session** (08 7.4; ID-T16; Ruling for docs/DECISIONS.md). The app calls `createExtensionSession` with `client_component = share_ext` after login and when the shared keychain has no valid child tokens at start; it stores the child's access and refresh tokens in the shared keychain group (`AfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly`, not synchronizable); a new child replaces the old one on the server. `ExtensionSession` in `ClientKit` uses the access token while valid and refreshes the child family with the same DPoP key when it expires; the refresh is single flight inside the extension process and persists the new refresh token before using the new access token (the M4-09 rule). The app never refreshes the child family; the extension never touches the app's family. A `401 session_revoked` or reuse rejection deletes the child tokens and turns the send into an inbox entry.

4. **Send and inbox** (15 12; 04 13.2; Ruling for docs/DECISIONS.md). The extension generates `client_msg_id` (UUIDv7) when the person taps Send; uploads each file (create, parts, complete) and waits until `upload_state = complete`; then `createMessage` with `client_msg_id`, the comment and the attachments, or `createThread` with `first_message` for "new post". Any failure (no network, timeout, `5xx`, `429`, missing or revoked session) writes an inbox entry: a folder in the App Group with the files and a `manifest.json` holding `client_msg_id`, target, comment, created time, upload IDs already completed, never tokens. A `4xx` other than `401` and `429` (for example `403` because write permission is gone, `413` too large) shows the reason in the sheet and keeps nothing. The extension completes the request when the send or the inbox write is done.

5. **Takeover by the app** (15 6.1, 7.5). At start and when coming to the foreground the app reads the inbox manifests, inserts each into `share_inbox` and the outbox with the same `client_msg_id` (uploads before the message, 15 7.5), and deletes the folder after the outbox has taken the files into its own storage. A replay that the server answers with the original message (duplicate `client_msg_id`) completes the entry. The takeover is idempotent (an entry already in `share_inbox` is skipped).

6. **Android** (15 12; 01 5.4). Intent filters for `ACTION_SEND` and `ACTION_SEND_MULTIPLE` with `text/plain`, `image/jpeg`, `image/png`, `image/heic`, `image/webp`, `application/pdf` and the other concrete types the upload accepts per 12 (list them explicitly; no `*/*`). The activity hands the content URIs to Flutter through Pigeon, the Flutter route shows the same target sheet as the extension (recent targets from the app's database, search through the same operations), and the send goes through the app's outbox (uploads, then the message, 15 7.5). Content URIs are copied into the app's cache through `ContentResolver` streams before the activity finishes.

7. **Recent targets** (01 14 `client.share.recent_targets`). The app writes the last `client.share.recent_targets` targets the person sent to (from any platform's send path) into the App Group as `{target kind, id, display path}`; display paths are channel and thread names the person can see. Accept this as the only names in the shared container (15 12 requires it); they are removed on logout and on `access.changed` that removes a target.

8. **Rulings for `docs/DECISIONS.md`** (`D-M5-06-<n>`): the sheet and search sources (note 1); copy to the App Group and JPEG conversion (note 2); child session handling (note 3); send, failure classes and inbox format (note 4); idempotent takeover (note 5); Android MIME list and route (note 6); names of recent targets in the shared container and their removal (note 7).
