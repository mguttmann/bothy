# M3-22: Push registration and preview endpoint (ADR 0031)

Status: ready | Depends on: M3-05, M3-06, M3-07, M3-20, M3-21, M3-23 | Milestone: M3

## Goal

After this ticket a person's app registers the push target of its own active device with `registerPush` and gets, exactly once, a preview token that is bound to that registration, to the session that registered it and to the device key; the server stores only its HMAC in `push_registrations.preview_token_hash` (13 6.4, R-PN-07). A known `(provider, token)` is taken over by the new registration, because tokens move between installations (13 13). `unregisterPush` removes a registration of the person. The Notification Service Extension (M5-04) and the Android push service (M5-05) load the content of a hint from `GET /api/v1/push/preview` (`getPushPreview`) with `Authorization: DPoP <preview token>` and a DPoP proof of the device key, never with a refresh token: the server checks token, registration, session, device and person, then `message.read` on the message and `push.deliver` on its thread with the rights of the moment of the fetch (13 1 point 2, 05 4.9 row `push.deliver`, ADR 0031). A revocation between send and fetch gives `404`, identical to an unknown message; `notify.preview_mode` decides what the answer carries (`full`: names, excerpt and the first image as a fetch ticket of M3-05; `names`: names only; `none`: no names, only kind, thread, message and badge). The same preview builder fills the preview fields of the ephemeral event `notification.created` (13 6.3) in the job `notify.gateway_send` of M3-23, by the recipient's `notify.preview_mode`. `auth.logout`, `device.revoke` and `user.disable` deactivate the registrations in their own transaction through the in-transaction events `identity.SessionEnded` and `identity.DeviceRevoked` (`disabled_reason` `logout` or `device_revoked`): from the commit on the preview token is worthless and the job `notify.push_send` of M3-19 sends nothing to that device (13 14 row "`auth.logout`, `device.revoke`, `user.disable`", ID-T22 and ID-T26 for their push step). The registrations of a device appear in the API schema `Device`. The canary scenario of M1-69 carries a push token and a preview token, so neither ever reaches a log, a span, a metric, an audit row or a problem body (SEC-T10). This closes the server side of "Push without content" in M3: the hint pipeline (M3-19) and the providers (M3-20, M3-21) now have targets that apps can create, and a content path that never passes Apple or Google.

Product requirements implemented here:

11. `push.register` registers a push target of the caller's own active device and returns the preview token exactly once; resource `device`, right `self`, no audit, `rl.write` (05 4.9, 13 13). The row binds `user_id`, `device_id` and `auth_session_id` of the caller's session (03 `push_registrations`).
12. The preview token is issued once, stored only as HMAC in `preview_token_hash`, rotated with every new registration and valid only for `getPushPreview` (13 6.4, 03 principle 9, R-PN-07); it is DPoP-bound to the device key and has no refresh (R-PN-07, 08 7.4).
13. A known `(provider, token)` is taken over by the new registration, so it stops reaching its former owner (13 13); the unique key `(provider, token)` of 03 holds.
14. `push.unregister` removes a registration of the caller; a foreign or unknown registration gives the same `404` (05 4.9, rule 2, B7).
15. The preview fetch is valid only while the registration is active, the device is `active`, the registration's session is not revoked and the person is active (13 6.4); then `authorize(r, message.read, message)` and `push.deliver` on its thread with current rights; no right, a deleted or an unknown message gives `404` (13 6.4, 05 4.9 row `push.deliver`, P3).
16. The answer follows `notify.preview_mode` with the default `notify.default_preview_mode`: `full` with names, excerpt (plain text, mentions as names, at most `notify.preview_max_chars`), attachment count and `image_url` (a fetch ticket for `small` of the first image, 12 9.2); `names` without excerpt and attachments; `none` only `kind`, `thread_id`, `message_id`, `badge` (13 6.4 table).
17. `auth.logout` deactivates the registrations of the session with `disabled_reason = 'logout'`; `device.revoke` deactivates all registrations of the device with `device_revoked`, at once; `user.disable` deactivates the registrations of the person (13 6.4, 13 7.3, 08 10 step 2, 08 11 step 5). Afterwards the preview token is worthless and no push reaches that device (13 14).
18. Device tokens and preview tokens never appear in logs, spans, metrics, audit details, events or problem bodies (13 7.3, 13 10, SEC-LOG-4, SEC-SEC-3, SEC-T10); the preview fetch is the receipt of 13 9 and counts `bothy.push.fetch` per provider.
19. `notification.created` carries the preview fields of 13 6.3 (`space_name`, `channel_name`, `thread_title`, `sender_display_name`, `excerpt`, `attachment_count`, `image_url`) by the recipient's `notify.preview_mode`, built by the same preview builder as the preview endpoint, except `image_url`, whose fetch ticket needs a session (Design note 9 f); `none` gives none of them (13 6.3, 13 6.4 table, D-W-108 point 8).

2. **Observability** (signals.md 4.4, 13 9, 17; Ruling for docs/DECISIONS.md). Declare `bothy.push.fetch` as a `Spec` in the registry of M1-68 (counter, unit `{notification}`, attribute `bothy.push.provider`, role `api`) and count one per preview request whose token identified an active registration (steps a to c of Design note 8 passed), whatever the answer after that (`200`, `404`, or `401` from the session check), with the registration's `provider`. A malformed or unknown token counts nothing, because no provider is known. This is the receipt t2 of 13 9 and the numerator of the "Confirmation rate"; the server keeps no hint record, so it measures no t2 minus t0 latency (report item). Logs, spans and errors of this ticket never carry a device token, a preview token, the `Authorization` or `DPoP` header, a ticket URL, a name or a text (SEC-LOG-3, SEC-LOG-4, SEC-SEC-3); span attributes only `bothy.push.provider`; error texts are fixed strings.

3. **The preview token** (13 6.4, ADR 0061, 08 12; Ruling for docs/DECISIONS.md). Form `bothy_pvw_<random>_<checksum>` from `domain/apitoken` of M1-44 with the new kind `pvw`: 256 bits from `crypto/rand` in Base62 as exactly 43 characters, CRC-32 (IEEE) over the random part as exactly 6 Base62 characters. `Tokens.NewPushPreviewToken` returns it in the redacting secret type of M1-44 with `Hash` = HMAC-SHA-256 with the pepper of `auth.token_pepper_ref` over the full token string, the derivation of `api_tokens.token_hash`. `Tokens.HashPushPreviewToken` checks form, kind `pvw` and checksum and returns `ErrTokenMalformed` before any HMAC for every other value (reuse `ErrTokenMalformed` if the merged code has it, otherwise create it with a doc comment). The Authenticator of M1-34 and M1-44 answers a `pvw` token under `Bearer` or `DPoP` on any operation with `401 unauthenticated` without a database call, and `createToken` never derives the kind `pvw`. Reason for this form and not opaque bytes: the gitleaks rule `bothy-api-token` finds a leaked preview token (SEC-SEC-4), and the checksum rejects junk before the database (ADR 0061 point 2). The token is valid only through `getPushPreview` (Design note 12) and only as long as its registration row is active; it has no expiry of its own and no refresh (R-PN-07): it ends with its registration, its session, its device or its person.

4. **Validation and provider policy** (13 5.1, 5.2, 5.4, 13 13; 06 5 points 3 and 5; Ruling for docs/DECISIONS.md). `internal/wire` builds `RegistrationPolicy{Enabled map[string]bool; APNsTopics []string; FieldMaxBytes int}` from `apns.Config.Enabled()` (M3-20), `fcm.Config.Enabled()` (M3-21), `push.apns.topics` and `push.registration_field_max_bytes`, in every role, without building an adapter or reading a secret (the role `api` serves `registerPush`). After `authorize` (Design note 5 a), in this order:

    a. Provider `unifiedpush` or `wns`: `400 validation_failed` with `errors[].code` `not_enabled` at `/provider` (13 5.4: no adapter in this server version).

    b. Provider `apns` or `fcm` that is not enabled on this instance: `403 feature_disabled` with `reason` `push_provider_disabled` (06 5 point 5: a switched-off function answers authenticated principals so that the app can show why).

    c. Field rules, all collected into one `400 validation_failed`: the device's `platform` must be `ios` or `macos` for `apns` and `android` for `fcm`, otherwise `invalid` at `/provider`; `environment` absent means `production`, and `sandbox` with `fcm` is `invalid` at `/environment` (only APNs has two environments, 13 5.1); an `apns` token consists only of hexadecimal digits in an even number and is stored in lower case (the form M3-20 sends in the path), an `fcm` token only of printable ASCII without white space, otherwise `pattern_mismatch` at `/token`; `token` and `app_id` longer than `push.registration_field_max_bytes` are `too_long`; an `apns` `app_id` must be in `push.apns.topics`, otherwise `invalid` at `/app_id` (M3-20 would disable it as `topic_mismatch` at the first send).

    The new configuration key `push.registration_field_max_bytes` (integer, default `1024`, minimum `64`, maximum `2048`, so a token always fits the B-tree entry of the unique key `(provider, token)`): in `internal/wire/configkeys_push.go`, in 13 17 and through `make config-reference` in the reference. Reason: the schema has no length for both fields, and an oversized token would fail the unique index with an internal error.

5. **`registerPush`** (13 6.4, 13 13, 06 7; Ruling for docs/DECISIONS.md), one unit of work:

    a. `authorize(actor, push.register, device {ID: actor.DeviceID, OwnerID: actor.ID})` with the rules of Design note 6; then `identity.Devices.Get` for the device's `platform`; then Design note 4.

    b. Lock order of 03 6.3: the registration rows of one statement in ascending `id`.

    c. Takeover: `LockRegistrationByProviderToken(provider, token)` (`FOR UPDATE`); a row, active or disabled and of any person, is deleted (`DeleteRegistration`). 13 13 says "the old row is disabled", but the unique key `(provider, token)` of 03 covers disabled rows too, so an old row and the new one cannot both exist; deleting has the effect of deactivating for the former owner (no push, its preview token hash is gone) and keeps no device token in two places. Write this into 13 13 (English). Pending `notify.push_send` jobs of the deleted row end with `no_registration` (M3-19).

    d. One current token per app installation: `DisableOtherRegistrationsOfApp(device_id, provider, app_id)` sets `disabled_at = now` and `disabled_reason = 'token_invalid'` on the other active rows of this device with the same provider and `app_id`, because a provider gives one installation one current token and the app reports the new one; otherwise the device would get every hint twice until the old token fails.

    e. Mint the preview token (Design note 3) and `InsertRegistration`: `id` UUIDv7, `user_id` = `actor.ID`, `device_id` = `actor.DeviceID`, `auth_session_id` = `actor.SessionID`, `provider`, `token`, `environment`, `app_id`, `preview_token_hash`, `created_at` = now; `failure_count` 0.

    f. `idempotency.MarkOneTimeSecret(ctx)` and `201` with `PushRegistrationCreated` (the registration and `preview_token`). A replay with the same `Idempotency-Key` answers `409 conflict` with `reason` `one_time_secret` (06 7, R-API-26); the app then registers again with a new key, which rotates the token.

    g. Two registrations of one `(provider, token)` at the same time: the second insert hits the unique key after the first commits; the unit of work runs once more from step c, and a second unique violation answers `409 conflict`.

    h. No durable event and no audit entry (05 4.9: Audit "no", no event in 05 6); the person's other clients see the registration in `Device.push_registrations` on their next read.

    i. `unregisterPush`: `GetRegistrationForOwner(id)` gives `user_id`; `authorize(actor, push.unregister, push_registration {ID, OwnerID: user_id})`; unknown and foreign give the same `404 not_found`; then `DeleteRegistration` and `204`. A second call answers `404`. Removing frees the token for a later registration and ends its preview token.

    a. Credentials from the context (Design note 12): exactly one `Authorization` value with the scheme `DPoP`, otherwise `401 unauthenticated` with `WWW-Authenticate: DPoP algs="ES256 EdDSA"`.

    b. `Tokens.HashPushPreviewToken`: `ErrTokenMalformed` gives `401 unauthenticated` without a database call.

    c. `GetRegistrationByPreviewHash` with `disabled_at IS NULL` (the unique partial index of 03): no row gives `401 unauthenticated` with `WWW-Authenticate: DPoP error="invalid_token"`. A deactivated or deleted registration answers exactly like an unknown token.

An actor whose `DeviceID` differs from `reg.DeviceID` gives `401 unauthenticated`.

    e. `identity.DPoP.Verify(ProofRequest{Proofs, Method, URL, AccessToken: raw preview token, ExpectedJKT: actor.KeyThumbprint})` with `URL` the `htu` the chain computed: a `DPoPError` gives `401` with its code (`invalid_dpop_proof` or `use_dpop_nonce`) and the `DPoP-Nonce` header (`errs.WithHeader`); `NonceStale` adds `DPoP-Nonce` with `CurrentNonce()` to the answer. `ath` binds the proof to the preview token (RFC 9449), so a proof for another token fails.
