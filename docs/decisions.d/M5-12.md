| D-M5-12-1 | 2026-10-27 | Extra keys: Ctrl and Alt are off, one-shot or latched. A tap on an off modifier makes it one-shot; a second tap within Flutter's double-tap interval (`kDoubleTapTimeout`) latches it; any later tap turns it off; a long press latches it at once (screen readers and switch access cannot tap twice in time). One-shot clears after the next key from the bar or the keyboard, latched stays. Ctrl with a letter or one of `@[\]^_` sends the control byte, with any other key the key unmodified; Alt puts ESC in front of the next character or key of the bar; the arrows send `ESC [ A` to `D`, or `ESC O A` to `D` while the renderer is in application cursor mode (DECCKM); `|`, `~`, `/` and `-` send themselves. The keys of the bar go to the renderer's output directly, past the paste guard. One-shot shows an accent border and the label "Control, for the next key", latched the filled accent and "Control, latched". This replaces the long-press-only latch of D-M4-35-12. | 01 5.3 M8 ("tapping twice holds them until the next tap"), brief Design note 2; the long press stays as the accessible way. |
| D-M5-12-2 | 2026-10-27 | Keyboard privacy: on iOS and Android the renderer reads the hardware keyboard only and the soft keyboard goes through `TerminalTextInput`, a text input client with suggestions, autocorrection, smart dashes, smart quotes, personalized learning (`IME_FLAG_NO_PERSONALIZED_LEARNING`), autofill and the delta model off; Android gets `visiblePassword`, iOS the plain text keyboard (its `visiblePassword` is the ASCII keyboard without the person's languages and dictation, R-PR-11). The field holds a two-space sentinel and is reset after every change, so it never keeps terminal input; a deletion sends backspace, return sends return, a composition is sent when it ends. Residual risk for the threat model: a third-party keyboard may still record what is typed, which the app cannot prevent. | xterm 4.0.0 attaches its own client with smart dashes and quotes on and no way to change its configuration; brief fixed requirement 15, Design note 3. |
| D-M5-12-3 | 2026-10-27 | Capture and app switcher: while M8 shows, Android keeps `FLAG_SECURE` on the window in front and behind on every API level (no screenshot, recording or recent apps picture; `RecentsProtection.setTerminalSecure`), and leaving the last M8 hands the flag back to the recent apps rule of D-M5-03-5 (a link from one host's M8 to another builds the new screen before the old one is disposed, so the screens are counted). iOS hides the terminal behind a notice while `UIScreen.isCaptured` (recording, mirroring, AirPlay), told by `IosScreenEvents.captureChanged`; the app switcher image shows the privacy cover of M5-02, drawn on `inactive`. Residual risk: iOS cannot block a screenshot; the screenshot notification arrives after the picture, so the app does not use it. | R-PR-12, brief Design note 4. |
| D-M5-12-4 | 2026-10-27 | Detach and reattach order: the move to the background (`hidden`, `paused`) and the app lock's `locked` event detach every live session (the stream closes with `1000`, the session keeps running). On the return the coordinator decides after the lock answered the same life cycle event: while the lock is closed or still deciding, nothing attaches; once it is open (or the person passed E6, `unlocked`), the sessions detached by the coordinator attach with `attachTerminalSession`, a new ephemeral key and handshake, and the replay replaces the screen. The server decides the window (`terminal.quick_reattach_window`); a `401 step_up_required` runs the step-up through the device key with biometrics once and repeats; a failed attach shows its reason and "Reattach" without a retry loop. A session the person detached, ended, or that the server or another device changed meanwhile is not attached again. | Brief Design note 5; 08 6.3 item 5, 24 6.2. |
| D-M5-12-5 | 2026-10-27 | Leaving M8 detaches the host's live sessions, by system back on Android as by the back button and the edge swipe on iOS; only "End" or the server ends a session. | 01 5.4, brief fixed requirement 12. |
| D-M5-12-6 | 2026-10-27 | A hardware keyboard hides the bar.
