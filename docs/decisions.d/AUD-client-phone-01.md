# AUD-client-phone-01 Phone: terminal sessions after a left screen, logout confirmation, waiting targets and lost photos

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| AUD-CP1/1 | 2026-11-12 | `PhoneTerminalCoordinator` attaches a terminal session of the phone again only while the terminal screen M8 of its host shows, the app is in front and the app lock is open. M8 tells it when it shows and when it goes (`screenShown` when M8 builds, `screenGone` when it goes); the coordinator remembers a session it detached only while its M8 shows and attaches it only then; `screenShown` runs the check again, so a returning M8 gets its sessions. When M8 goes while the lock holds the content, E6 only replaced it: its sessions stay remembered and attach when M8 is back with the lock open. When it goes any other way (back, a tapped push, a link, a share) its sessions are forgotten and stay detached until the person attaches them. The detaching is not bound to M8: the tabs of a host follow the life cycle from their first M8 until the login ends, as before this ticket, so every live session detaches in the background and at `locked` (R-PR-12, 01 5.6 E6), also one that turned live after its M8 was left (AUD-CP1/7 (a)); such a session is not remembered and stays detached. This refines D-M5-12-4 and D-M5-12-5. A link that replaces M8 while the lock covers it is the one case the screen cannot tell from E6: the sessions stay detached while another page shows and attach when the person opens M8 of that host again. | Audit finding client-phone-background-4: the coordinator remembered the session when the app moved to the background on M8; a tapped push, a link or a share then replaced M8, nothing took the session out of the coordinator, and the next return attached it without a view, again at every later return, after `terminal.quick_reattach_window` with a step-up prompt over another page. The finding proposed to forget at every dispose of M8. That would end the reattach after the app lock, because E6 replaces the phone shell and with it M8 while the lock is closed (D-M5-12-4 keeps: the sessions attach after `unlocked`); the real app had no test of that path until this ticket. The first build of this ticket bound the detaching to a shown M8 as well. The review reproduced the gap: a session the reconnector attached after M8 was left stayed live in the background and behind the lock, which the code before this ticket did not allow. Only the remembering and the reattach are bound to M8 since. |
| AUD-CP1/2 | 2026-11-12 | The coordinator does not stop an opening or an attach that is on its way when the app moves to the background or the lock holds the content. It listens to the tabs it knows (AUD-CP1/1) and detaches a session in the same call in which it turns live while the app is in the background or the lock holds the content; a session whose M8 shows is remembered like every other one it detached, so it attaches on the return. When the lock closes, E6 replaces M8, and M8 stops the flow as it does whenever it goes (AUD-CP1/3): an opening is cancelled, and a stopped attach leaves the session detached and not remembered, so the person attaches it after the unlock. | Audit finding client-phone-background-8 (R-PR-12, 01 5.6 E6): such a flow ran to its end and left a live stream behind the background or E6. Stopping it there was considered and rejected: a flow may wait at a prompt (the warning of 24 8.4, a changed key, the step-up), which the controller cannot close; a stopped flow would leave a prompt without effect on the screen the person returns to, and the attach of the return would put a second prompt over it. A flow that lands in front after the return is correct as it is. The stream is closed before a frame of the host is read, so no terminal content arrives in between. |
| AUD-CP1/3 | 2026-11-12 | Leaving M8 stops what is on its way to a session: `TerminalController.stopPending` of `terminal_ui` ends an opening or an attach in the phases `preparing` and `connecting` (no further prompt, request or stream). A tab with a session is detached as "Detach" leaves it; a tab without one is idle, and a session the server opens for it meanwhile is closed right away, as for a closed tab. The controller's mark of a running flow belongs to that flow's generation, so the next attach starts at once while a stopped request still waits for its answer. M8 is left exactly once, by its pop or by its dispose, so a new M8 of the same host that shows while the old one still slides out is not stopped by the old one. The answer to a prompt of a stopped flow that is still open has no effect. | The systems deliver a tapped push after the app is in front: the coordinator has started to attach when the thread replaces M8. Without the stop that attach completed without a view (findings client-phone-background-4 and -8), and after the quick reattach window the server's `step_up_required` opened the step-up over the thread. The stop is the one change outside `apps/client/lib/phone`: a flow can only be ended by the controller that owns it (D-M4-35-17). |
| AUD-CP1/4 | 2026-11-12 | "Log out" of the phone's Me tab asks once (`confirmLogout`): the dialog says that the data of the device and its device keys are deleted and, when outbox entries wait, how many changes are lost (the count of the offline banner). "Cancel" and a tap outside keep the login. The button "Log out" of E6 and the one in the foot of the desktop sidebar are unchanged. | Audit finding client-phone-background-6: a tap by mistake deleted the outbox and both device keys, and the device needed a new login (04 8.5, R-SY-14). The finding is filed for the phone. The desktop foot lies in `apps/client/lib/sidebar`, which another work package of the fix program changes at the same time; it stays an open minor of that area (the same dialog, one call). |
| AUD-CP1/5 | 2026-11-12 | A thread or a channel of a tapped push or a link that the device does not hold while the server cannot be asked (`TargetStatus.unknown`) shows a progress mark (`PhoneTargetWaiting`) instead of an empty page. When the connection turns live in that state, the page asks the target check again and loads the thread again (the history loads an unknown thread only once per column, 15 11); a channel arrives with the sync. "Not found or no access" shows as soon as the server says so. No new text: the offline banner above the page names the state. A check that fails while the connection stays live is asked again only at the next reconnect. The desktop shell is unchanged. | Audit finding client-phone-background-10, verified at the source: `TargetStatus.unknown` promises "waits and tries again later", but nothing asked again, and the page stayed empty without a sign. A timer that asks again while the connection is live would be a retry loop for a server error and is not part of the finding. |
| AUD-CP1/6 | 2026-11-12 | The photos of a pick during which Android ended the app are deleted at the next start: `MediaPickerPort.takeLost` (the `retrieveLostData` of `image_picker`, asked only of its Android implementation), `MediaPicker.discardLost`, and the provider `lostPhotosDiscardedProvider`, which the phone shell of an Android build reads when it first shows, once per start. The photo is not attached to a message. A result the picker has not finished when the shell first shows is not found by that start. | Audit finding client-phone-background-12, verified at the source: `retrieveLostData` was never called, so the camera's file stayed in the app's cache, against requirement 16 of brief M5-07 (no photo outside the outgoing folder). Attaching it would need the composer and the thread of the ended process; the app restores no state after a process end, and state restoration is a feature of its own. Not proven on a device: the path needs an emulator with a camera and the developer option "Don't keep activities"; the tests hold the port contract with a fake and the call at the start. |
| AUD-CP1/7 | 2026-11-12 | Not covered, recorded as open: (a) a session whose stream broke while it was attached (detach causes `connectionLost`, `serverRestart`, `slowConsumer`, `hostUnavailable`) is still attached again by the reconnector of D-TSL-5 when the connection is live again or the app returns to the front, also after its M8 was left; it is then live without a view while the app stays in front with the lock open, and the next move to the background or `locked` detaches it for good (AUD-CP1/1). An attach from the hosts screen M9 ends the same way when the person goes back from M8 while M9 still loads the sessions of the host (`HostsScreen`, read in the code, not run); (b) the foot of the desktop sidebar logs out without asking (AUD-CP1/4); (c) a target check that fails while the connection stays live is asked again only at the next reconnect (AUD-CP1/5); (d) the photo of a pick that Android ended the app during is deleted, not attached (AUD-CP1/6). | (a) is the same end state as finding client-phone-background-4 by another way, found while building and not part of the audit: the reconnector is shared with the desktop (`apps/client/lib/terminal`), reattaching a lost stream is the decision D-TSL-5 (15 23.2 item 6), and limiting it to a shown M8 on the phone needs its own ruling on the return after the app lock. The other three are the limits of the rulings named. |
