# FIX-keyboard-dismiss-focus-ring The keyboard closes on a tap outside, and no yellow ring on text fields

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| FIX-KD/1 | 2026-11-02 | One widget, `DsKeyboardDismiss` of the design system, installed once in `MaterialApp.builder` of the client around the navigator, closes the keyboard for every text field of the app (composer, search, dialogs, settings): it overrides `EditableTextTapOutsideIntent` so a tap outside the focused field unfocuses it on every platform, also a touch on iOS and Android, and on iOS and Android it sets `ScrollViewKeyboardDismissBehavior.onDrag` on the ambient scroll behavior. The composer wraps itself in a `TextFieldTapRegion`, so attach, voice, the caller's control, send and the suggestion list count as part of the field. The desktop keeps `manual` for scroll views. | Owner report of 2026-11-02 on an iPhone: neither a tap outside the field nor scrolling closed the keyboard; only leaving the screen did. Flutter's default tap-outside action unfocuses on a touch only on the web, and every scroll view defaults to `manual`. One place covers screens and dialogs alike. On the desktop a trackpad scroll is a drag of the scroll view, so `onDrag` there would end the focus of the composer while the person scrolls the history. |
| FIX-KD/2 | 2026-11-02 | The focus ring is in the theme's accent in both themes: dark `color.focus.ring` aliases `palette.spruce.300` (as `color.accent.fg`) instead of `palette.gorse.300`, so it is swapped with the instance accent like the light ring; `color.focus.ring-on-sidebar` is `palette.spruce.300` in both themes instead of `palette.gorse.300`. Buttons and other controls keep the ring of `border-width.focus` (WCAG 2.4.7); every pairing passes the contrast gate. | The owner found the thick yellow ring ugly (screenshots of 2026-11-02, dark theme). Its source: `design/tokens/color.dark.tokens.json` (`focus.ring` and `focus.ring-on-sidebar` on `palette.gorse.300`, `#D9C42B`), emitted as `DsAccent.dark*.focusRing` and `DsColors.*.focusRingOnSidebar` and drawn by the input decoration theme of `packages/design_system/lib/src/theme/ds_theme.dart` (`focusedBorder`, width `border-width.focus`), by `DsFocusRing` and by `DsTappable`. |
| FIX-KD/3 | 2026-11-02 | A focused text field shows the caret and an outline of `border-width.hairline` in `color.focus.ring` (`inputDecorationTheme.focusedBorder`); the composer sets its decoration's `focusedBorder` to none and turns the hairline of its own field to `color.focus.ring` while focused. `DsFocusRing` still draws no ring around an `EditableText`. | The composer has `border: InputBorder.none`, but `focusedBorder` came from the theme, so a 2 px ring was drawn inside the composer's field ("Follow-up to" in the thread view). The caret is the focus indicator of a text field; a hairline in the accent keeps a subtle, consistent outline in light and dark. |
