| M1-65/15 | 2026-10-17 | One rule for delivery across the phases of a connection: (a) from `Register` until it goes live a connection is a candidate of every event of a space or channel, so its live buffer holds everything it may be entitled to and the flush decides with the rights of that moment; (b) an event with a seq at most the head of the connection is not sent again (the snapshot or the catch-up covers it), but a `channel.created` among them still adds its channel to the hub indices; (c) a removal of `access.changed` at seq N drops only queued, buffered or paged events before N. The live buffer stays bounded by `sync.outbound_queue_max`. | The rounds before fixed one instance of the same class each: events lost, sent twice or out of order across registering, catching up, live and revoked when space, channel and rights changes ran concurrently. Red team round 4 reproduced a default operator agent that lost the first message of a channel created while it caught up; the model test also found a lagging tail sending events up to the head twice, and a removal processed by the tail after the pages had read a later re-grant dropping entitled events. Class test `TestHub_DeliveryModel` (seeded random runs of the real hub with fakes, red on `d498e61`); regressions `TestHub_OperatorNewChannelWhileCatchingUp`, `TestHub_NewChannelUpToHeadIsLearned`, `TestHub_LiveSkipsTailEventsUpToHead`, `TestHub_StaleRemovalKeepsLaterPageEvents`, `TestHub_StaleRemovalKeepsQueuedLaterEvents`, `TestQueue_PurgeOnlyBeforeTheRemoval`, `TestGateway_OperatorNewChannelWhileCatchingUp` (real database). |
