# M4-51 Sidebar ordering

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-M4-51-1 | 2026-10-31 | The sidebar has three levels with an order of their own: the spaces, the channels and forums of one space, and the open posts of one forum. Each level is in the person's manual order or arranged by activity. Threads of a text channel keep 01 6.8 items 2 to 4. | Owner request 2026-10-31 names exactly these levels and three independent switches; text channel threads are few, bounded and hang off messages of the main history. |
| D-M4-51-2 | 2026-10-31 | The manual order of every level is personal data of 04 14, synchronized to every device of the person and invisible to others: spaces by `user_space_states.sort_key`, the channels of a space by `user_space_states.channel_order`, the posts of a forum by the new `user_channel_states.thread_order`. The collapse of a space stays per device (R-PR-24). | 01 6.7 and R-PR-24 name the sidebar order as data that crosses devices; the space order already syncs, and a person with 25 forums arranges them once, not on every device. |
| D-M4-51-3 | 2026-10-31 | The channel and post orders are lists of IDs. Rows in the list follow it; rows not in it come first in the level's default order (channels: the global order; posts: last activity, newest first). A move writes the whole list of the level's rows the client holds in the new order, in one write; the spaces keep their fractional keys. | One write per move with the last write wins of 04 14; a forum or chat created later appears where the person sees it; the list stays bounded by what exists. |
| D-M4-51-4 | 2026-10-31 | An automatic level is ordered by last activity, newest first: a post by `last_activity_at`, a channel by the newest activity of its threads the client holds (its main history included), else its creation time, a space by the newest of its channels. Ties keep the manual order. While the person is in the sidebar an automatic level keeps the order it showed last (01 6.8 item 4). | The owner wants a written chat to rise with its forum and space; holding the order keeps the promise of M4-46 that no row moves under the pointer. |
| D-M4-51-5 | 2026-10-31 | The switches are the personal settings `sidebar.auto_arrange_spaces` (default `false`), `sidebar.auto_arrange_channels` (`false`) and `sidebar.auto_arrange_chats` (`true`) of 01 11, synchronized like every personal setting, shown in Settings, Appearance on the desktop and the phone; a default deletes the key. | 01 11 makes personal settings synchronized and the order they switch follows the person; `chats` on keeps the order of M4-46 for everyone who never opens the settings. |
| D-M4-51-6 | 2026-10-31 | Desktop rows are dragged with the mouse and a line shows the drop; on a touch screen a long press lifts a row (also a space, which before started at once and competed with scrolling). A row stays in its parent. At an automatic level a row does not lift and a note names the switch. Replaces D-SB-7 for channels and forum posts. | The owner asked for drag and drop with an indicator on the desktop and long press on the phone; a drop into another parent would be a move, which stays the dialog with its checks. |
| D-M4-51-7 | 2026-10-31 | The sidebar menu of a space, a channel and a forum post has "Move up" and "Move down", disabled at the ends, and at an automatic level disabled below "Arranged by activity"; the rows carry the same two screen reader actions. | The keyboard and screen reader alternative to dragging, as the spaces had since M5-10. |
| D-M4-51-8 | 2026-10-31 | `putMySidebarSpace` takes an optional `channel_order`; the new `putMySidebarChannel` takes the `thread_order` of a channel; both check only the form (unique UUIDs, at most `sidebar.order_max_items`, default and maximum 1000, the `maxItems` of the API, so no answer leaves its schema), not whether an ID names a row the caller may see; the event `sidebar_channel.updated` and the snapshot member `sidebar_channels` carry the channel rows. | Checking each ID would tell the caller which hidden channels or posts exist; the client ignores IDs it does not hold. |
| D-M4-51-9 | 2026-10-31 | In a manual forum the "Show more" limit takes the first `ui.sidebar_forum_posts_visible` posts of the order, and a move places the post among the shown rows while the posts behind "Show more" keep their place. | A move must never drop or reorder posts the person cannot see at that moment. |
