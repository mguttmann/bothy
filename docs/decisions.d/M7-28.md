| D-M7-28-4 | 2026-10-27 | Metadata rule (Design note 4): a showcase file keeps exactly what a decoder needs. PNG keeps IHDR, PLTE, tRNS, IDAT, IEND and the colour chunks sRGB, gAMA, cHRM; every other chunk (tEXt, zTXt, iTXt, eXIf, tIME, iCCP, pHYs, private chunks) and bytes after IEND are metadata, and stripping rebuilds the file from the kept chunks with their CRCs. MP4 metadata are udta, meta (ISO and QuickTime form), ilst, every uuid box, free or skip with a non-zero payload, and the creation and modification times of mvhd, tkhd and mdhd; stripping turns each metadata box into a free box of the same size with a zeroed payload and zeroes the times in place, so stco and co64 offsets stay valid.
