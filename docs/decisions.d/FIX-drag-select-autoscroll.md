# FIX-drag-select-autoscroll Selection that scrolls

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-FIX-DSA-1 | 2026-11-02 | Cause in the history: each message had its own `SelectionArea` around its content (`DsMessage`, M4-50), below the history's `ListView`. Flutter scrolls a list while a selection drag is held beyond its edge only when the selection sits above the list's `Scrollable`; below it, a drag ended at the edge of the message and the history never moved. Fix at one place: one selection above the list on the desktop (`DsListSelection`), each row a `DsSelectableRow` that keeps everything but the content of a selectable `DsMessage` out of it. This supersedes D-M4-50-2 (one selection per message). | Owner report 2026-11-02 (macOS app): a drag that reached the bottom did not scroll, so only the visible part could be selected and copied. |
| D-FIX-DSA-2 | 2026-11-02 | A message's part of a selection exists only while the message is built, and the history is a lazily built list. While a selection lasts, `MessageList` widens the cache extent of the list from the view back to where the selection started and as far as the list scrolled since (in steps of half a view), so every message of the range stays built, also after a wheel scroll before shift and a click; the cache returns to its default when the selection ends. Kept alive rows (`KeepAlive`) were rejected: Flutter gives a kept alive row a zero paint transform, so its selection could no longer be ordered or extended. A selection model of message ids and text offsets independent of the widgets was rejected for this fix: it would replace Flutter's selection painting, word and paragraph clicks and keyboard copy. | The copy must be what the person selected; a widened cache costs memory only while a selection exists. |
| D-FIX-DSA-3 | 2026-11-02 | Messages in a copy are apart by an empty line: the selection container of a message's content adds it when a message lower on the screen has selected content too. | Flutter joins neighbouring selectables with nothing; the blocks of one message already use an empty line (D-M4-50-3). |
| D-FIX-DSA-3a | 2026-11-02 | A word or paragraph select (double or triple click, the right click of macOS) outside the content of a message selects nothing. | With one selection over the whole list, Flutter would select the word nearest to a click on an avatar or a name, and the message menu would offer "Copy" (01 6.15 item 2). |
| D-FIX-DSA-4 | 2026-11-02 | The scroll at the edge of the history and of a code block without wrap is Flutter's own (`EdgeDraggingAutoScroller` of the `Scrollable`): it starts once the pointer is beyond the edge and grows with the distance. "Copy" of the message menu copies the whole selection of the history, from whichever message the menu opened. | One mechanism per list; the menu is the one menu of a right click (D-M4-50-4). |
| D-FIX-DSA-5 | 2026-11-02 | Cause in the terminal: xterm 4.0.0 (`TerminalGestureHandler.onDragUpdate`) selects from the screen position where the drag began to the pointer, both mapped through the current scroll offset, and never scrolls during a drag. The view now handles the mouse drag itself (`TerminalMouseSelection`): the start is a cell of the buffer, a drag held within one line of the top or bottom edge or beyond it scrolls the scrollback at a speed that grows with the distance beyond the edge zone up to a bound (`TerminalAutoScroll`), and the selection follows the pointer; xterm's own update during the drag is replaced. Shift with a click extends a selection from its far end. A press of the mouse focuses the screen, since a drag is no tap and xterm focuses only on a tap. Touch keeps xterm's long press. | Owner report 2026-11-02, right after using the terminal. A terminal scrolls its scrollback while a selection is dragged at its edge in every desktop terminal. |
| D-FIX-DSA-6 | 2026-11-02 | Tests: widget tests under macOS and Windows for the history (drag held above and below with the oldest and newest messages built only during the drag, the copy shortcut and "Copy" of the menu, shift and a click after a wheel scroll), for the list selection of the design system (messages apart by an empty line, a code block taller than the list, a code block scrolled sideways) and for the terminal (scroll up and down with the copy in order, faster farther out, shift and a click after a wheel scroll); iOS and Android keep no selection in the history. No GUI test on a maintainer's machine, so the feel with a real trackpad is not proven by them. A click alone (an empty selection) followed by shift and a click far away keeps only the rows built at that moment. | Same limits as D-M4-50-6. |
