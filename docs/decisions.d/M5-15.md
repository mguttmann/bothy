The screen reader part holds for the whole page, not for the focused row: Flutter tells the app only on the focused node itself where the screen reader focus is, and on M1 that focus is in the list or on the tab bar one step from it; a page on top of M1 (M2, M3, the new chat page) ends the hold, so the rows are current when the person returns. | Brief Design note 1; a hook per row would need every row widget of the design system to report focus. |
| D-M5-15-2 | 2026-10-28 | The new chat view is a page of the Spaces stack on top of M1 (`/c/<forum>/new` gives the pages M1 and the view, also with `?entry=` while a sent post waits). Back leaves it as every page leaves: the iOS back button and edge swipe, the Android up arrow and system back pop the page, the router goes to M1, and the view's dispose deletes an empty draft through the controller of M4-41 (a draft with text stays and shows as the draft row). On the echo of the sent post the post's history replaces the page with the stack M1, M2, M3. | Brief Design note 2, requirement 12; the controller and draft handling stay shared, so a draft started on the phone is the desktop's draft row and back. |
| D-M5-15-3 | 2026-10-28 | M1 marks no selected row on the phone: the phone shell does not keep the route selection, so a thread row is never "selected" there and the selected-first priority of 01 6.8 item 3 does not apply on M1 (the open thread is on screen as M3, not next to the list). | The list is a page of its own on the phone; a row selected while its history covers the list would, on return, jump out of the group when the selection clears. |
