| M1-31/9 | 2026-10-12 | `addOwner`, `revokeOwner` and `enableUser` authorize once per request in a first unit without rights change (also loading the resource and checking reason and state), then write in a second unit declared as the rights change, which locks the person (the owner changes also the active owners rows, in ascending id) and checks the state again but not the authorization. Every rights change is serialized by the lock on `permission_version` of the rights begin hook, so I1 and SR8 hold for `enableUser` without the owners lock. Two owners revoking each other at once give one `204` and one `409 last_owner`. A raced `enableUser` that finds the person already active answers as without effect (no event); inside the Idempotency-Key stage the rights version of that attempt may have been raised. | A request is judged by the rights at its start (R-RE-02, one rights version per request); SR8 and I1 guard the result under the lock (test 5 of the brief). A refused call never raises the rights version. |
