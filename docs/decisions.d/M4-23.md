| D-M4-23-5 | 2026-10-24 | Quick switcher: rows are spaces, text and forum channels, threads (main threads are represented by their channel), people (users except the signed-in person) and agents, and the commands of the instance, the current space and the current channel; DM channels are reached through their participant's row, which opens the stored DM whose other participants are exactly that person or agent, and otherwise only closes (the client never creates a DM).
