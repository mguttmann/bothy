| D-M6-10-2 | 2026-10-15 | Distance: OKLab Euclidean distance of the two simulated colours (`delta_e_ok`), computed on the unrounded simulated values. `cvd_min_delta_e_ok` is 0.05 in `design/design-rules.json`, with source and reason there: 2.5 times the just noticeable difference 0.02 of deltaE OK (CSS Color 4), because chart marks are small and the noticeable difference grows as marks shrink (Szafir 2018). A row passes when the distance rounded to three places reaches the minimum, as contrast rows round to two.
