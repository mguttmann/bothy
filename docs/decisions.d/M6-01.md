| D-M6-01-2 | 2026-10-16 | Cookie `__Host-console_session`, 32 bytes of `crypto/rand` as base64url without padding, stored as HMAC-SHA-256 with the token pepper; attributes `Path=/; Max-Age=<seconds to expires_at>; Secure; HttpOnly; SameSite=Strict`, no `Domain`.
