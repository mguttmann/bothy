# M3-16 Search load test

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-M3-16-1 | 2026-10-24 | The search load test is `services/server/perf/search` (`TestPerf_Search`, build tag `perf`) with its untagged library `perf/internal/searchbench` and the seeding and product checks `perf/internal/searchworld`; the tool of the plan row is the make target `bench-search`, which runs only that test and prints its two targets (`perfreport -required search.p95_target,search.prefix_p95_target`). `bench-search` is in neither `gates`, `check` nor the pre-push hook. Place: root `Makefile`, `perf/internal/report/perfreport/main.go`. | 02 section 6 puts load tests under `perf/` with the shared report; Go's `internal` rule keeps `perf/internal/report` out of reach of `tools/`, and a second results file would contradict M1-72. |
| D-M3-16-2 | 2026-10-24 | `search.p95_target`, `search.prefix_p95_target` and `search.benchmark_messages` get the place `Load test` in a new column `Place` of 14 section 15 (every other row `Server`): no environment name, not in the configuration schema. `perf/internal/report` reads the `Load test` rows of the tables "Configuration values of this chapter" of 02 and 14 (`report.LoadAll`, each section by its title); a name in both chapters fails, a missing row or an unparseable value fails at lookup. A meaning with the word `target` is a target, a lower bound with `(minimum)`, else an upper bound; every other `Load test` row is a parameter. Both search targets join `report.Required`; a result can carry a `note` (the corpus size) that the table prints. Place: `perf/internal/report/report.go`, `threshold.go`, `results.go`. | 02 section 22 already treats `perf.*` this way; the three values are read by no server process. |
| D-M3-16-3 | 2026-10-24 | The parameters of the run are the named constants of `perf/internal/searchbench/constants.go` (world, corpus, load, query mix; values in `perf/README.md`, copied into `extra.search.params`); `DefaultParams` feeds `TestPerf_Search`, `SmallParams` the small-corpus tests with the same code. | 14 section 7 fixes languages, Zipf, spaces, channels and a partial view, nothing else. |
| D-M3-16-4 | 2026-10-24 | Rights, people and channels come through the public API (`searchworld.Seed` on a seeding process with every `ratelimit.*` raised); the content rows (`threads` of the posts, `messages`, the counters of every thread) come from a bulk loader through the admin pool of the throwaway database while no server runs on it: `COPY` of the posts, `COPY` of the messages in batches of `LoadBatch`, one `UPDATE` of the main threads, then `VACUUM (ANALYZE)` of `messages` and `threads`, then the count and the per-thread aggregate check. Ids are UUIDv7 from `ids.NewGenerator` at each row's `created_at` with a seeded ChaCha8 reader, one generator per id column; `threads.version` grows by one per message and per deletion as the product's `AppendToThread` and deletion do; `client_payload_sha256` is the SHA-256 of the text as the brief fixes it (a replay of a loaded `client_msg_id` is not part of the test). `searchworld.CheckThreads` compares `ConsistencySamples` threads with `getThread` and `listThreadMessages` before the measurement and `searchworld.CheckWrites` writes one message per language and one post per language after it; the small-corpus tests in `make test` compare every thread. Place: `perf/internal/searchbench/loader.go`, `perf/internal/searchworld/check.go`. | Building `search.benchmark_messages` messages through `createMessage` would use most of the time limit and measure the write path that `perf.message_create_p99` holds; the checks show that the rows equal what the product writes. |
| D-M3-16-5 | 2026-10-24 | `VACUUM (ANALYZE)` of `messages` and `threads` after the load and before the measurement. | The GIN pending list is merged and the planner has statistics, as on an instance where autovacuum has run. |
| D-M3-16-6 | 2026-10-24 | Measured server: the seeding process restarted on the same durable database with the default configuration and the limits of the profile mode (`perfkit.Env.Measure`). Load: every searcher open loop at `BudgetShare` of `ratelimit.rl.search.per_minute`, the searchers phase-shifted evenly (`perfkit.Schedule`, the one scheduler of M1-72 and M3-24), no background writes; phases one after the other, each `Warmup`, `Window`, up to `Drain`. Measuring point: from the scheduled send time to the fully read body on loopback in the test process, nearest-rank p95. Before each phase every person renews its token pair (`refreshToken`), so no sample pays a renewal. Place: `perf/search/search_test.go`, `perf/internal/searchworld/searchworld.go`. | 14 section 7 names the target "server duration without network" but no load and no measuring point; the scheduled send time avoids coordinated omission. |
| D-M3-16-7 | 2026-10-24 | Query mix of phase `messages`: `GET /api/v1/search?q=` with the defaults of `openapi.yaml`, German or English with equal chance, classes one, two and three words, phrase and negation `a -b` by their shares; the words of a query come from one frequency band (frequent, medium, rare) chosen by share; a phrase is two adjacent words of a visible live message, at least one a content word. Phase `prefix`: one or two words of the title of a visible post, each cut to between `search.prefix_min_chars` and its full length. Place: `perf/internal/searchbench/queries.go`. | Frequent words reach `search.max_candidates` (R-SU-04), and their share lies above the five percent a p95 may ignore. |
| D-M3-16-8 | 2026-10-24 | A measurement counts only when the path behaved correctly: a status other than `200` (among them `429 rate_limited` and `503 unavailable` with `reason` `search_timeout`), a body that does not decode strictly into `SearchHitPage` or `ThreadTitleHitList`, a hit of another kind or from a channel the searcher may not read, a phrase or prefix query without a hit, a probe answer with an item, `candidates_capped` or `next_cursor`, a failed load, product or rewrite check, or fewer than `MinSamples` samples fail the test and mark both targets failed. Failure messages name phase, class, query, counts and ids, never content. Place: `perf/internal/searchbench/checks.go`, `perf/search/search_test.go`. | ADR 0063 point 5 and T-AZ-02: a fast but wrong or leaking path must not end green. |
| D-M3-16-9 | 2026-10-24 | Phase `probe` has no threshold: the searchers without the probe space send the probe term (only in channels of the probe space) and the absent term (nowhere) alternating, `ProbeWarmup` of each unmeasured and `ProbeQueries` of each measured; both must give the empty page, and `extra.search.probe` records p50, p95 and their differences. | 14 4.3 accepts the residual risk and names no bound; RK-10 is rated on the measured values. |
| D-M3-16-10 | 2026-10-24 | Phase `rewrite`, after the measured process stopped: sizes first (table, indexes on `messages.search_vector` and `threads.title_search`, database, the share of the search indexes), then in one transaction `DROP COLUMN` and `ADD COLUMN ... GENERATED ALWAYS AS (<expression read from the catalog>) STORED` and the saved index definitions without `CONCURRENTLY`; afterwards expression and index definitions equal the saved ones and a fixed phrase finds the same messages. `extra.search.rewrite` holds the values and the values per million messages; the upgrade guide turns them into the maintenance window, no fixed duration or size stands in documentation. Place: `perf/internal/searchbench/rewrite.go`, `docs/operations/upgrade.md`. | The duration depends on host and corpus (14 section 8, RK-38). |
| D-M3-16-11 | 2026-10-24 | PostgreSQL of the load test runs with the defaults of its image and the durability of `pgtest.Durable()`, without tuning; `extra.search.postgres` records `shared_buffers`, `work_mem` and `maintenance_work_mem`. | 16 prescribes no tuning for `bothy-postgres`, so the shipped database runs with the image defaults too. |
| D-M3-16-12 | 2026-10-24 | `make perf` already passes `-p 1` (M1-72), so no change there; `PERF_TIMEOUT` stays as it is. | The packages run one after another; see the report of M3-16 for the measured duration. |
