# FEAT-SPACE-CHANNEL-CREATE Create a space and a plain channel in the app

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-STRUCT-CREATE-1 | 2026-11-23 | "Create channel..." in the space menu shows when the stored access to the space has `manage`, the right of `channel.create` (05); unlike "Create team channel..." it needs no owner. "Create space..." shows for an owner (`getMe`), the right of `space.create`: in the space menu and as a button below "No spaces yet" in the empty sidebar. Both on the desktop and the phone, where the sidebar and the space menu exist. Before the space's access is reported the channel entry stays hidden. | The entries offer only what the server allows, so none of them can only fail; the empty state is the only place a fresh installation can start from. |
| D-STRUCT-CREATE-2 | 2026-11-23 | Both creates are online calls through `StructurePort` (`createSpace`, `createChannel` with kind `text`) with one `Idempotency-Key` per name, not outbox entries, like the create of a team channel (D-TEAM-CREATE-2). A refusal drops the key; a lost answer and `409 request_in_progress` keep it, so the next press is the same request; a changed name takes a new key. Cancel is disabled while a call runs. | The dialog must show a refusal at once and needs the new ID to open the channel. Keeping the key on `request_in_progress` avoids a second space or channel while the first request still runs (06 4); the dialog of "Create team channel..." still drops it there, a known minor of that dialog. |
| D-STRUCT-CREATE-3 | 2026-11-23 | After "Create channel..." the app goes to `/c/<id>` of the new channel, like "New project" after its forum; after "Create space..." the dialog closes and the space shows in the sidebar once the sync delivers `space.created`. Only the name is asked: icon, color and the place in the order keep the server's defaults, the topic stays empty. | The route waits for the sync to store the channel, so opening it costs nothing more; a space has no page of its own to open. |
| D-STRUCT-CREATE-4 | 2026-11-23 | The German labels say "Bereich" for a space ("Bereich anlegen...", "Neuer Bereich"), not "Space" as the issue's wording had it. | The glossary of 01 section 9 and every other German label of the app translate space as "Bereich". |
| D-STRUCT-CREATE-5 | 2026-11-23 | Not in scope: renaming a space or a channel (`space.update`, `channel.update`), the topic of a channel, a forum channel from this dialog (forums come from "New project" and "Add forums for existing folders") and screens in the admin console. They stay API operations. | Owner scope of the ticket: the fix is the dead end of a fresh installation. |
