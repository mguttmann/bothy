# M5-09 Emergency actions on mobile

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-M5-09-0 | 2026-10-25 | Built on `main` after M4-27 and M5-03 landed; the changelog entry and these rulings are fragments. Differences to the brief's names, following the merged code: the administration of M4-26 and M4-27 has no separate controller classes; its "controllers" are the data source `AdminRepository` and the state mixin `AdminActionState`, which the emergency views reuse. The phone's pages of the area live next to the entry in `apps/client/lib/phone/me/emergency_entry.dart` (`PhoneEmergencyPage`, `PhoneEmergencyPersonPage`) under the existing phone routes `/admin/:section` and `/admin/:section/:id` as `/admin/emergency` and `/admin/emergency/{user_id}`. The confirmation is a bottom sheet (`EmergencyConfirmSheet`) instead of the desktop's `ConfirmChange` dialog. The phone wireframe is M13 of 01 5.3, after M12. | D-W-100 (merged names win over the brief's). |
| D-M5-09-1 | 2026-10-25 | Reuse, not rebuild: `EmergencyHome` and `PersonEmergencyView` in `packages/admin_ui/lib/src/emergency/` run every list and action through `AdminRepository` (`people`, `person`, `devicesOf`, `revokeDevice`, `disablePerson`, `revokeGrant`) and keep busy state, notices and refused controls in `AdminActionState`; the device rows are the `DeviceTile` of M4-25. The one addition to the data layer is `AccessRepository.grantsOf` (`listGrants` with `subject_kind` and `subject_id`, all pages), because a person's grants were only reachable per target; the fake server's `listGrants` reads the same two filters. | 15 3 `admin_ui`; R-CL-01; brief Design note 1. |
| D-M5-09-2 | 2026-10-25 | Confirmation and step-up: each action opens one sheet with the plain-language text from `l10n` that says it takes effect at once (`emergencyRevokeDeviceBody`, `emergencyRevokeGrantBody`, `emergencyDisableBody`), a reason field (required for `revokeGrant`, which requires one; the other two send the administration's default reason when it is empty) and a confirm button that names the step-up, because 05 gives `device.revoke`, `grant.revoke` and `user.disable` step-up: "Confirm with Face ID" when the theme's platform is iOS, "Confirm with fingerprint or screen lock" otherwise. The button only names what follows: the request goes out at once, and the `StepUpInterceptor` of M4-09 runs the device key step-up on `401 step_up_required` and repeats it with the same `Idempotency-Key`. A cancelled step-up sends nothing more, leaves the page unchanged and says "Cancelled. Nothing was changed."; a `403` hides the action with the server's reason; `409 last_owner` (SR8) shows the problem text and how to resolve it on the desktop or in the console, the client never blocks it beforehand. Stopping a run keeps the stop of M4-21 and M5-02 in the live box and Running now unchanged: `run.cancel` has no step-up and no confirmation there (D10, the `/stop` command), so the phone asks nothing more for it. | 07 9.5, SR8; 08 6.2, 14.3; brief Design note 2, requirements 12 and 13. |
| D-M5-09-3 | 2026-10-25 | Entry rule: "Emergency" shows under Me for an owner (`getMe`, `is_owner`, the provider `isOwnerProvider` the app already has) or for a person whose stored space access, as the server reported it in the `SpaceView` of the snapshot and of `access.changed`, holds the capability `manage` (`emergencyRoleOf`). No grant is read. An owner sees the person's devices with "Revoke" and "Disable person"; a manager only the grants the server lists for them, and the devices are not requested (`device.list` is self or owner). A direct link into the area for anyone else shows "Not found or no access" without a request. The person search sends the server's `q` filter of `listUsers`. | 01 4; 05 `device.list`, `grant.read`, `user.disable`; 15 1 principle 1; brief Design note 3. |
| D-M5-09-4 | 2026-10-25 | Exactly four actions on the phone: revoke a device (`device.revoke`), revoke a grant (`grant.revoke`), disable a person (`user.disable`) in the area, and stop a run (`run.cancel`) where it already is. Every other path under `/admin` stays "Not found or no access" on the phone, and the Me tab's entries are a closed list (the settings sections, "Emergency", "Log out"), which a test walks. Creating grants, the audit log, statistics and group editing stay on the desktop and in the console. | R-PR-07; brief requirement 11. |
