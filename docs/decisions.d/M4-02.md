| D-M4-02-5 | 2026-10-15 | Read retry only for `GET` and `HEAD`, after a network error, a timeout, `429` or `503` except `503 setup_required`; the wait is `Retry-After` (seconds or an HTTP date) or a full-jitter backoff `uniform(0, min(cap, base * 2^n))`. `ReadRetryPolicy.fromBackoff(base, cap)` sets `maxAttempts` to one plus the number of doublings of `base` until the bound reaches `cap` (base 2 s and cap 5 min: nine attempts), so the apps pass only `client.outbox_retry_base` and `client.outbox_retry_cap` and no new key exists. `createApiClient` therefore takes a required `readRetry` parameter, which the brief's signature did not list. A retry runs through the whole interceptor chain again (a new span ID, a new proof from the auth slot). | Brief Design note 4; 15 8: the outbox retries writes. |
