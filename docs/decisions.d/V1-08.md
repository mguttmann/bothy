# V1-08 `apps (iOS)` green: the walk through Photos

Plan row V1-08: the job `apps (iOS)` of `ci.yml` passes on `main`. The open failure was the device
test `share.share_image_from_photos_test` ("an image from another app lands in the chosen
thread") with `Photos shows no Share button`.

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-V1-08-1 | 2026-11-06 | Cause, read from the job logs: the walk through Photos ran out of its bound (`nativeWait`, 3 min), not into a different Photos. Every round asked Photos one query per view; the query for the grid cells (`PXGGridLayout-Info`) took 15 to 25 s, because patrol resolves each matching element on its own. The first tap on the newest photo did not put a Share button on screen (the query for `Share` was empty 2 s and again about 20 s after it), and the walk, which still saw the grid's cells in the tree, tapped the photo a second time after two more rounds; right after that second tap the Share button was there. In the passing job the walk reached Share after 161 s of its 180; in the failing jobs, with one more round for the welcome sheet and its prompts, the second tap came at the bound. | PR run 42178688293 jobs 127763141712 and 127782084893 failed, main run 42162235973 job 127710508350 passed, all three on `macos-15-arm64`, Xcode 16.4, simulator iPhone 16 Pro with iOS 18.5, with the same sequence of steps. The runner image is not the cause: the failing rerun 127782084893 ran on image version 20261104.0514.1, the same as the passing job, and job 127763141712 on 20261028.0489.1. The tree Photos showed at the failure holds the viewer with Back, More and Share (taken right after the second tap). Whether the first tap opened the viewer without its bars or did not open it is not visible in the logs, because patrol cuts its log lines; the walk of D-V1-08-2 handles both. |
| D-V1-08-2 | 2026-11-06 | The walk reads one snapshot of the whole tree of Photos per round (`getNativeViews` without a selector, with Photos as installed app) instead of one query per view, and decides from it in `readPhotosView` (`integration_test/share/support/photos_walk.dart`, unit tests in `test/share/photos_walk_test.dart`). The viewer of a photo (`OneUpMainPagingView`) comes before the grid: when it shows no Share button on screen in two rounds in a row, the walk taps the middle of the viewer, which shows its bars (or hides them, and the next tap shows them again). The viewer is never idle and never swiped; a round that answered a prompt does not count towards the two rounds before a photo is tapped. Rounds are 500 ms apart. A failure names the frames of what Photos showed. | A snapshot takes a fraction of a second where the grid query took up to 25 s, so a missed tap costs a round of about a second instead of 40 s, whichever layout Photos shows. The grid stays in the tree under the viewer, so reading the viewer first keeps the walk from tapping a grid cell behind it. The test is neither skipped nor weakened, and no runner image is pinned. |
