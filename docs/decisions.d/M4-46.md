# M4-46 Sidebar shows all open forum posts

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-M4-46-1 | 2026-10-29 | A forum group in the sidebar lists every open post the client holds, ordered by `last_activity_at`, newest first (ties: creation time, then ID); a done post shows only while it is selected. Unread, mentions and the run state icon stay on the rows. Text channels keep D-SB-2 and D-SB-3 (window, bound with priority, order by creation). Replaces D-SB-2 and D-SB-3 for forums. | Owner decision 2026-10-29: "like Discord"; after an import most open posts had no activity within `ui.sidebar_thread_window` and a forum with 24 open posts showed none. A text channel's threads hang off messages of the main history, which stays reachable; the brief keeps their rule. |
| D-M4-46-2 | 2026-10-29 | The limit is the new App key `ui.sidebar_forum_posts_visible` (default 15). Beyond it the row "Show more" ("Mehr anzeigen") above "All posts" lists every open post in place and then reads "Show less" ("Weniger anzeigen"); its screen reader label adds the number of posts it shows or hides. The choice is kept per device in `channel_view_states.posts_shown_in_full` (`local_store` schema version 9, false in every kept row), never synchronized. The selected post shows also below the limit. `ui.sidebar_thread_window` and `ui.sidebar_threads_per_channel` keep their names and now apply to text channels only. | App keys are compiled defaults (no stored configuration to migrate), so redefining the two keys needs no migration, while a rename would retire names that older briefs and rulings use. A "Show less" row is the only way back without a menu; 01 11 has no setting key for a view preference (like D-SB-6). |
| D-M4-46-3 | 2026-10-29 | While the sidebar holds its rows (pointer or focus in it, 01 6.8 item 4) a forum group keeps the order of the rows shown last; posts new to the group come first by activity. "Show more" and "Show less" ignore the hold and apply at once. | Ordering by activity was rejected in D-SB-3 because rows would move under the pointer; freezing the order while the person is in the sidebar keeps that promise. The toggle is the person's own request, so holding the old rows would make "Show less" look broken. |
| D-M4-46-4 | 2026-10-29 | "All posts" keeps the counters of every post the group does not show, also of the posts behind "Show more"; "Show more" carries no counters. The new design system row is `DsSidebarMoreRow`. | One place for the marks of what is not on screen, as D-SB-4 and D-SB-5 require (no number twice, nothing unread out of view). |
| D-M4-46-5 | 2026-10-29 | The snapshot rule of 04 6.3 item 4 (`active_threads`: unread, running and up to `sync.snapshot_threads_per_channel` posts active within `sync.snapshot_thread_window`) is not changed. The sidebar lists what the client holds: the snapshot, the posts that events brought (an import that runs while the client is connected or catches up arrives this way) and the pages of the forum overview. | The reported case is the client filter; posts the client stores but did not show. Sending every open post of every forum in each snapshot changes the sync contract and the snapshot size for every client; that is a decision of its own, recorded as an open point in the brief. |
