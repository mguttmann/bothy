| D-M6-06-1 | 2026-10-24 | Data per section of A7: `getRunStats` gives the run tile (the total of `runs`; a second call for the previous range of the same length gives the comparison), runs by result per bucket (completed, failed, cancelled of every agent together), the run duration per agent and the failure reasons (`failed_reason_<code>` summed); `getCostStats` is called twice, `group_by=agent` (tiles and costs per agent) and `group_by=space`; `getToolCallStats`, `getErrorStats` and `getUsageStats` give their sections, the usage always in weeks because `active_people` exists only per week or month (D-M6-05-3). Only counters and the amounts of one billing mode are summed over buckets; rates, percentiles and counts of people are shown per bucket as delivered. The duration chart shows p50 and p95 of the latest bucket per agent, its table every bucket, because a percentile of the range cannot be built from bucket percentiles. Bucket sizes follow the range: hours up to `statsHourlyUpTo` (two days), days up to `statsDailyUpTo` (92 days), weeks above. The server widens every range to whole buckets (hours in UTC; days and weeks from local midnight of `stats.time_zone`, 23 or 25 hours around a clock change), and the client cannot reproduce that without the zone, so `getInstance` reports it as the new member `stats_time_zone` (spec first) and every range is built on those buckets: the 24-hour preset on whole UTC hours, the day presets and custom ranges as calendar days of that zone (`StatsDays`, over `L10nFormat.dayStart`). "Last 7 days" are today and the six days before it, the previous range the seven days before those, also across midnight and a change of daylight saving time; nothing is widened, and a bucket present in both answers (possible only with week buckets of a long custom range) counts for the current range only. The tile "waiting for people" is left out: `listActiveRuns` has no state filter, and the brief forbids deriving it from a statistic. | Brief Design note 1; the cut-offs are presentation (bars stay readable), the limits stay the server's (`stats.max_range`, `api.stats_max_buckets`). |

A value named in `below_min_group` reads "fewer than the minimum group" without a number, because `InstanceInfo.limits` has no `privacy.stats_min_group_size`. With `currency_required` the screen loads the first named currency and offers the others. | Brief Design note 2; admin-statistics.md 2 and 3; 25 4 rule 5 and 7.4. |
