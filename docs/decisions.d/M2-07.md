| D-M2-07-4 | 2026-10-17 | Takeover order under a per-process lock per consumer: save the acknowledgement of the older connection of this process, `Take` with the new gateway session as holder (creating the row at `ready.head_seq`), close the older connection with `4015 consumer_replaced`, drop a REST window of the key, log one INFO line with agent, consumer and both session ids. `4015` is not resumable, so the older session leaves the session store and its `resume` gets `4006`. The gateway ignores `cursor` and `sync_epoch` of an agent's `identify` and `resume`; a `resume` first proves with a fenced save that the session still holds the row (otherwise it closes with `4015` and the newer holder stays, also when the takeover happened while the session was detached, through a REST poll or in another process), then continues from the acknowledgement held in the session and closes a still open older connection of the session with `4015` instead of `4017`. | Brief note 4; 04 15.1 items 3 and 4 (the durable cursor decides), agent connections have no device. |
| D-M2-07-5 | 2026-10-17 | The window (`gateway/domain.ConsumerWindow`) marks exactly the ack-timeout redeliveries with `redelivered: true`; the deadline runs from the oldest unacknowledged dispatch. | D-M2-07-6 | 2026-10-17 | Consumer connections pull from the log up to the hub position (`Hub.Position`: the highest seq the log tail of the process has passed through `Observe` and `Dispatch`, seeded with the head the tail started at); the hub queues no durable dispatch for them and wakes their runners after each event. The pages are read in filter mode with a request scope of their own per page (the rights version is read once per page), not in delivery mode. | Brief note 6 for the bound. | D-M2-07-7 | 2026-10-17 | Acknowledgement rules on both transports: `seq` below 0 or above the head is `validation_failed` on `/seq`; `seq` at or below the acknowledgement changes nothing and is no error; a higher `seq` covers every number up to it, also beyond what was dispatched. | D-M2-07-8 | 2026-10-17 | The head that consumer operations report and check against is the hub position of the serving process; only a gateway connection creates a missing cursor at the `head_seq` of its own `ready`. | D-M2-07-9 | 2026-10-17 | A REST poll is a consumer like an `identify`: per call, under the per-key lock, a gateway connection of the key in this process is saved and closed with `4015`, `Take` writes the holder of the REST window (a UUIDv7 created with the window; the first poll creates the cursor at the hub position and answers an empty page), the window is reconciled with the stored acknowledgement, the ack timeout applied and the resync checked; `retention` and `cursor_ahead` answer `410 resync_required` with the reason after moving the cursor to the head. `next_cursor` is the window's position but never covers an event an earlier poll handed out that is still unacknowledged (it stays below the oldest one), so a lost response cannot acknowledge events away; they come again after `agent.ack_timeout`. `getConsumerState` and `ackAgentEvents` take nothing over; `ackAgentEvents` writes at once without holder condition and tells a window of the key in this process. | Brief note 10; R-SY-09 holds for both transports. |
