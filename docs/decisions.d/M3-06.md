| D-M3-06-1 | 2026-10-20 | `files.scan` keeps its read pass; for `clean` and `skipped` its final transaction leaves a JPEG, PNG, GIF or WebP `upload_state = 'pending'` and inserts `files.thumbnail` with `InsertTx`; MP4 and QuickTime files become `complete` and get the job; other types and blocked or failed files get none. | D-M3-06-2 | 2026-10-20 | The JPEG filter keeps every marker T.81 needs to decode (`SOI`, `DQT`, `DHT`, `DAC`, `DRI`, `DNL`, `DHP`, `EXP`, `SOF0` to `SOF15` except `JPG`, `SOS` with its data and `RST0` to `RST7`, `EOI`), the first `APP0` `JFIF` rewritten with the same version, units and densities but without its embedded thumbnail, every `APP2` `ICC_PROFILE` part in input order, `APP14` `Adobe`, and one new `APP1` with only "Orientation"; `JFXX`, other `APP0`, every other `APPn`, `COM`, `JPGn`, reserved markers and every byte after the first `EOI` fall away. Place: `adapters/imaging/jpeg.go`, 12 7.1. | An embedded thumbnail can show what a crop removed, the reason EXIF thumbnails fall away in ADR 0047; the decoder needs exactly the T.81 markers. |
