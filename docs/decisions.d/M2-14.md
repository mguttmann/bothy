| D-M2-14-11 | 2026-10-21 | The pure rules of `authz/domain` take domain types: `ActingForModeOf(action string)`, `ActingForApplies(mode, ActingForFacts{RunID, ThreadID}, Path, ResourceFacts, delivery)` and `CheckActingFor(status, mode)`; authz reads the binding through the optional interface `authorize.ActingForBinder` (`authorize.ActingForOf`), which `actor.Actor` implements with plain values. A principal without the binder whose `ActingForPerson` is set, and an `ActingFor` with a person but without a status, count as bound. Place: `authz/domain/actingfor.go`, `kernel/authorize/authorizer.go`, `kernel/actor/actor.go`. | `authz-domain` may depend on nothing but itself and `kernel-core`, and kernel ports never import each other (`.go-arch-lint.yml`), so the brief's signatures with `actor.ActingFor` and `authorize.Resource` cannot compile there; a narrowing is never lost (fail closed). |
