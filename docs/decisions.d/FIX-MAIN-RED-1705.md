# FIX-MAIN-RED-1705 Main red after #1705: an audit without a verdict and a Hermes start with a failed tool import

Main run 43082397581 (push of 10b17309e, the merge of #1705, 2026-11-11) failed in two jobs,
`apps (iOS)` and `test (SDKs and plugins)`. Neither failure comes from the change of #1705 (the
design system: text weight and the edge of controls), and the pull request run of #1705 itself
(43073060389) passed both jobs. The first is explained: a start condition of a loaded runner
that the test took for a result. The second is explained up to its sign in the log; how that
sign leads to the failed test is open (FMR1705/4).

The same run has a third job that is not green, and this ticket does not change it: `apps
(macOS)` was cancelled at its limit of 60 minutes. Its tests had passed (`make apps-platform`
ended after 57 minutes 47 seconds); the limit fell into the cache step after them. The job was
cancelled at the same limit in main run 43028486729, earlier that day and before #1705, and its
three passing main runs of that day took 55 to 60 minutes. It needs a ruling of its own (a
higher limit after PL/6, or fewer entries in the job as in PL/44).
