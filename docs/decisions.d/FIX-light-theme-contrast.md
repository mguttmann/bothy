# FIX-light-theme-contrast The phone's Spaces page in the light theme

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| FIX-LTC/1 | 2026-10-31 | Cause: the design tokens are correct. The sidebar is a brand surface in both themes (D-DS-02), and every `text.on-sidebar*` and `category.*.on-sidebar` role is paired only with `surface.sidebar*` in `design/design-rules.json`. On the desktop the column layout paints `surface.sidebar` behind the sidebar view, but the phone's Spaces page (`PhonePageView` with `SidebarColumn(phone: true)`) had nothing behind the view except the page frame in `surface.canvas`. In the light theme that put spruce 50 to 300 on granite 0 (1.07:1 to 1.35:1); in the dark theme the texts stayed readable on the dark canvas, which is why only the light theme looked broken. | Reproduced by `apps/client/test/phone/spaces_contrast_test.dart` over the real phone shell: no `surface.sidebar` above the rows and the text contrast guideline failed with 1.07 and 1.35 in light. |
| FIX-LTC/2 | 2026-10-31 | The fix is in the view, not in the tokens: `SidebarView` paints `surface.sidebar` itself, so it never depends on its host. The phone's Spaces page therefore stands on the brand surface in both themes, like the desktop sidebar; no token, no palette value and no generated file changes. The column layout keeps its own sidebar color for the column area. | One place that holds the pairing for every host; changing the on-sidebar roles to dark text in the light theme would break the desktop sidebar and D-DS-02. |
| FIX-LTC/3 | 2026-10-31 | Why the tests missed it: the phone scenes of the goldens and the accessibility tests and the desktop sidebar goldens wrapped `SidebarView` in a `ColoredBox` of `surface.sidebar` that the product never had. The wrappers are removed, so the goldens `phone_m1_spaces_*`, `m1_threads_*`, `android_m1_spaces_*`, `sidebar_*` and `draft_row_*` in light and dark now show the surface the view paints; their images are unchanged pixel for pixel. | A test scene must not supply what the product lacks. |
| FIX-LTC/4 | 2026-10-31 | New checks: `packages/design_system/test/theme/token_contrast_test.dart` checks every pair of `design/design-rules.json` (text 4.5:1, large text and `ui` 3:1) on the generated `DsColors` constants the app compiles, in light and dark and for every accent of `DsAccent`, and fails when a rule names a role the Dart theme lacks or a text role is in no pair. `spaces_contrast_test.dart` checks on iOS and Android, in light and dark, that the rows of the real Spaces page stand on `surface.sidebar` and (on macOS, D-M4-07-10) that its texts pass the text contrast guideline. | The generator gate checks the token sources; the new test holds the Dart values the widgets read, and the widget test holds the pairing at run time, which no token check can see. |
| FIX-LTC/5 | 2026-10-31 | Other screens checked in light: no widget outside the design tokens sets a fixed color (no `Colors.*` other than transparent and no `Color(0x...)` in `apps/client/lib` or `packages/*/lib`), the on-sidebar roles are used only inside the sidebar view, and the accessibility tests of the desktop sidebar, thread, composer, live box, settings and the phone screens, which run the text contrast guideline in light and dark on macOS, pass. The status bar strip above the phone's Spaces page keeps `surface.canvas` with the system's status bar icons for the theme. | Scope of the report: the whole app in light mode on all platforms. |
