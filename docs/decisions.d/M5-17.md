# M5-17 Phone thread density

| No | Date | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-M5-17-1 | 2026-10-31 | The phone layout of a message is the value of a new density token, `density.message-gutter`: 44 (avatar `size.avatar-md` plus `space.12`) in comfortable and compact, 0 in touch. Where the gutter holds the avatar it stays beside the content; a narrower gutter puts a small avatar (`size.avatar-sm`, initial scaled down to fit) at the start of the header line of a group's first message, and the content of every message, continuations included, starts at the row padding. The rows beside the content (files, thread starter lines, run lines, files of a waiting message) use the same token. | The touch density is used exactly on iPhone and Android, so a token states the rule in one place for the design system and the app; a flag would have to be passed through every row. The owner saw a quarter of the width lost to the avatar column. |
| D-M5-17-2 | 2026-10-31 | `DsComposer`: the horizontal padding of the text moves into the `TextField`'s `contentPadding` (`density.composer-pad-x`: 12 on desktop, 4 on touch), so the text field reaches from button to button and a tap on its padding focuses it; the text is explicitly left aligned; the vertical padding is `max(space.8, (density.target-min - line height) / 2)`, which centers one line on the buttons. A new `trailing` slot puts a caller's control inside the field before Send. | The decorator keeps a single line at the top of a field taller than the line (the 48 high touch target), which put placeholder and caret above the middle of the buttons; `textAlignVertical` had no effect there. On the desktop the values give the same layout as before (the desktop goldens of comfortable and compact did not change). |
| D-M5-17-3 | 2026-10-31 | The phone composer puts its press-and-hold microphone into `DsComposer.trailing` and offers the templates as the entry "Templates" of the attachment menu instead of a button beside the input; the insets are `density.composer-inset-x` and `density.composer-inset-bottom` (16 and 12 on desktop, 8 and 8 on touch). Microphone and Send stay two buttons. | With templates, attach, microphone and Send each 48 wide beside a padded field, the text field of a 390 point screen was about 140 points wide; now it is about 230. Swapping microphone and Send by the text state would change the voice flows of M5-07 and was not asked for. |
| D-M5-17-4 | 2026-10-31 | The phone's thread header is one line: the back button of the page (`PhoneHeaderBack`, label "Back to {page}", 48 square), the title in `type.heading` cut at its end, and the one overflow menu, whose first line is the operator ("Operator: Robin" or "No agent answers here") as text, not an action. The page frame of a thread (`PhoneHeaderBackFrame`) draws no back bar while a header claims the back action (`PhoneBackClaims`, counted after the header's first frame); a thread still loading or not found keeps the bar. | Before, the frame's back bar, a title that could wrap and the operator line took three rows. The claim keeps a way back on every page without a header, which an unconditional move of the button would have lost. |
| D-M5-17-5 | 2026-10-31 | The phone header collapses (height 0, out of the semantics tree, not hit testable) while the history scrolls towards older messages (`UserScrollNotification` with `ScrollDirection.reverse` of the reversed list) and shows again on `forward` and when the newest message comes into view, animated with `motion.duration-move` (0 with reduced motion). With `MediaQuery.accessibleNavigation` it never collapses. The live box does not collapse. | Reading back through a long Claude Code thread is where the height matters; a screen reader's focus order must keep the back button and the menu. The live box is one line and carries Stop, which must stay reachable (01 5.3 M3). |
| D-M5-17-6 | 2026-10-31 | `DsLiveBox.mobile` is one line: the state icon and one `Text.rich` of state word, run time and status line (inline code kept through `DsStatusLine.spans`), cut at its end, with the full status line in a tooltip; Stop at the end; the line's height is the touch target. The variant serves the desktop's collapsed active box too. | One text cut at its end shows the state first at every text scale; separate flexible parts cut the state word at a text scale of 200 percent in the first attempt (golden `live_box_final`). The sheet shows everything the line cuts. |
| D-M5-17-7 | 2026-10-31 | The golden of the phone thread (`phone_m3_dense`, `android_m3_dense`) is a scene of the real widgets (`ThreadHeader`, `DsLiveBox.mobile`, `MessageTile`, `DsComposer` with the phone's slots), as the other phone goldens of M5-02; the real `HistoryColumn`, `ThreadComposer` and page frame are held by the widget tests of `test/phone/thread_density_test.dart`. The semantics snapshots of M3, M4 and M7 lose the operator line of the header and gain attach and microphone inside the input. | A golden of the real column needs the database host, whose asynchronous setup the golden harness does not run; the geometry the owner reported is asserted on the real widgets. |
