| D-M6-13-1 | 2026-10-24 | The port `kernel/measure` (standard library and `kernel/ids` only) holds the 18 metric constants, the closed dimension lists and the scope (channel, agent) of 25 3.2, `Validate`, and `Value` with `Count`, `Sum`, `Elapsed` and `OK` (built by `One`, `Timed`, `Amount`, `Outcome`, `SLI`). The SLI thresholds are not injected into the kernel (`measure.SetBounds` of the brief is not built): the measuring point passes the duration and the outcome, and the stats recorder decides `good` with `stats/domain.IsGood` over `slibounds.gen.go`. An invalid call panics in a test binary (`testing.Testing()`) and is dropped and counted as `bothy.stats.flush.dropped{bothy.reason=invalid_key}` in production; signals.md 4.3b lists the value. | Modules still never import `stats` (02 4.4), and no process-wide mutable state is needed for the bounds; the threshold lives in one place, the recorder, which already depends on the generated file. |
| D-M6-13-2 | 2026-10-24 | `good` counts an SLI event whose outcome is good and whose duration is at most the threshold, the bound included (R-OB-06: `le` buckets are inclusive); without a threshold (`sli.api_availability`, `sli.push_acceptance`) only the outcome decides. Events that slos.md 2 leaves out of "all" are not recorded: a connection that ends with `client_error` (`sli.reconnect`), a send with `rejected_token` (`sli.push_acceptance`), a run that leaves `queued` as `cancelled` (`sli.run_start`), and the route `/api/v1/gateway` (`sli.api_availability`). `sli.api_availability` counts exactly the requests `http.server.request.duration` records, through `otel.ObservedMiddleware` (a panicking request is in neither). | Good and all are counted as the SLO and its histogram count them, so the traffic light and Prometheus agree. |
| D-M6-13-3 | 2026-10-24 | The recorder keeps counters per minute (UTC, the clock of the process at `Record`) and key in maps under one mutex held for one map update. The flush swaps the maps out under the lock and writes them outside it in chunks of at most `flushChunkRows` rows (internal/stats/app/flush.go), one `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT ... DO UPDATE` with additive `count`, `sum`, `good` and `updated_at = now()` per chunk on the pool, its own transaction. A failed chunk and every later one are merged back, marked as from a failed write; more than `stats.flush_queue_max` keys drop keys of the oldest minute first, counted as `queue_full`, or as `db_error` for counters of a failed write. The shutdown flushes once in the stop step `stats_flush` of the database phase, before the pool closes; when that flush fails, every key held is dropped and counted as `db_error`. The periodic flush runs every `stats.flush_interval` with a ticker. | 25 3.4: bounded, counted loss, never a wait of the domain path; chunks keep one statement and its transaction short whatever the bound; a chunk is committed or merged back as a whole, so nothing is added twice. |
| D-M6-13-4 | 2026-10-24 | Domain counters record in `txn.AfterCommit` of the unit of work that caused them, next to the existing OpenTelemetry instrument in the same function: `messages_created` (with the instrument `bothy.message.created`; a system line, origin `system`, is outside the dimension list and counted only in the telemetry), `threads_created` (threads created through `messaging.CreateThread`, the API and the gateway op; a main thread of a new channel and an imported thread are no such thread), `runs_started`, `runs_finished`, `sli.run_start`, `sli.turn_duration` and the `billing_mode_mismatch` part of `invocations_denied` (all with `RunTransitioned`), `cost` (with `bothy.run.cost` per booked entry in runs, the caller of the booking), `billing_mode_switches` (a change of the mode only; a new holder within `personal` is no switch) and `approval_cards`. `tool_calls` records after a committed patch next to `bothy.run.steps`. | D-M6-13-5 | 2026-10-24 | `approval_cards`: `requested` once per card message of an agent that carries at least one component with `required_capability = approve_actions`; `approved` for an interaction on such a component whose button value is `once`, `session` or `always`, `denied` for `deny` (the choice the allow audit records), nothing for any other interaction; `expired` once per card when the job `runs.component_expiry` expires at least one of its approval components unused. `invocations_denied`: `i2_denied` for every denial of `agent.invoke` that `bothy.agent.invocation.denied` counts (before a delivery, and at a token renewal), `billing_mode_mismatch` for a run that ends `failed` with that reason. Channel and agent are the card's or the run's. | The dimensions follow 09 11.5 and 10 (I2, I3) and the classification of the existing audit entry and counter. |
| D-M6-13-6 | 2026-10-24 | `stats_minutes` is partitioned per UTC day (`stats_minutes_YYYYMMDD`, no default partition); the migration creates none (upgrade.md M10). Partitions come from two SECURITY DEFINER functions of `bothy_owner`: `stats_minutes_ensure_partition(day)` and `stats_minutes_drop_expired(cutoff, lock_timeout)`. The job `stats.retention` runs hourly and once at the start of a worker (fixed schedule, no key), creates today's and tomorrow's partition and detaches and drops every partition whose day ends at or before `now - retention.stats_minutes` (plain `DETACH`, bounded by `database.lock_timeout`, because `DETACH ... CONCURRENTLY` cannot run in a function). A flush whose rows meet a day without a partition creates the partitions of the chunk's days and writes the chunk once more. | The flush never depends on the job for correctness, and the job stays a cheap hourly check; 24 runs a day keep tomorrow's partition ready long before midnight. |
| D-M6-13-7 | 2026-10-24 | `bothy config check` (cross-check `stats_retention_covers_slo_period`) fails when `retention.stats_minutes` is shorter than the longest `Period` of `slibounds.gen.go`; the message names the key and the SLO period, never the value. | 25 3.5, VZ-T14: the traffic light needs the whole SLO period. |
