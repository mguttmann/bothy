| D-M2-28-4 | 2026-10-24 | Matching: an entry for run R is matched when R has at least one valid entry with `occurred_at` in `[period_start, period_end)` and all of them have mode `organization`, the request's provider and the entry's currency; otherwise R is unmatched (no entry in the period, also an unknown or active run; `personal`; another provider; another or mixed currencies). A matched run whose valid entries in the period are all reconciled with the same sum is a no-op that counts as reconciled; another amount supersedes them. Entries outside the period stay. 10 12.4 states it. | The app submits the same days on every pass within `reconciler.lookback`, so a repeat must not grow the chain; `personal` amounts are information, never invoice (03); no conversion (10 12.1). |
| D-M2-28-5 | 2026-10-24 | Split within a run: share = floor(A times amount_j divided by the sum) over the valid entries in the period, the remainder to the largest amount, ties to the smallest ID; all amounts `0` put A on the smallest ID; every entry gets exactly one successor, also with `0`. Exact with `math/big`. An amount above `2^53 - 1` is `400 validation_failed` (`too_large`). | BR-10 one level down: one request entry carries one amount per run, a run has one entry per model; no estimate may stay valid next to a reconciled amount; amounts stay inside the integer bound of 06. |
| D-M2-28-6 | 2026-10-24 | The request stores the entries; the job `billing.reconcile` (args: the run IDs) applies them and reads its state again on every run; 02 12.3 says so. | D-M2-28-7 | 2026-10-24 | The job writes no durable event: no `run.updated` and no `agent_session.changed` for reconciled totals; `runs.version` and `agent_sessions.version` rise by one only for changed rows, and `cost_baseline_micros` stays.
