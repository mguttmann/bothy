| D-CU-04-10 | 2026-11-26 | Two signals of the terminal channel: `terminal.computer_open` (`host_id`, `session_id`; every node expects the session, the node of the control connection sends `computer.open`) and `terminal.computer_disable` (`host_id`; that node sends `computer.disable`). | D-CU-04-11 | 2026-11-26 | The computer relay holds at most two messages a side sends before its counterpart arrives (the first Noise message of `bothy-computer-mcp`) and writes them at the pairing; a further one, a text frame or a message above the Noise limit ends the session with `error` and `stream_fault`; the end of either side ends it with `connection_lost`, except a host data connection closed with `4101` or `4104` and a detail of 27 5.3, which ends it `open_failed` with that detail. | 27 5.4 and 6.1: no reattach. |
| D-CU-04-12 | 2026-11-26 | Openings that never paired are ended by `terminal.sweep` (interval `terminal.sweep_interval`) with `open_failed` and `open_timeout` once `computer.open_timeout` passed; the relay of the pairing node ends them at its own deadline before. | Brief Design note 5 (the job interval of a sweep for `opening` rows); no new job. |
